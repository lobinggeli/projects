How these images are made

  • The house plan is matched to the official cadastre (parcel 475, 8 cm average error), which puts the model in Swiss coordinates together with the swisstopo terrain, orthophoto and buildings.
  • Each drone photo has its camera solved on points of the house and the yard (roof corners, chimneys, manholes, drain): about 9 to 10 pixels of error on 2560. The view from the hill (photo 09) was tried and left out: the AI did not keep to the model there.
  • Each option’s model is rendered in Blender from that camera and set into the photo, only where something changes; the neighbourhood stays the photo.
  • An image-editing AI (Gemini) only makes the materials and light realistic, inside the changed area.

These are illustrations, not plans. Proportions come from the sketches (matched to about 20–30 cm). Colours, materials, furniture and visible interiors are assumptions or were added by the AI; the AI may also simplify details.
