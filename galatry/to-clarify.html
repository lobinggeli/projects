To clarify · realistic variant

The questions to put to the commune before the offer, for the realistic variant: full renovation, glazed south façade, lake-side terrace, no new garage. Under each block, what we already know.

How to use it: send the questions in writing to the BTI (the intercommunal technical office that handles permits for Corseaux), with a sketch of the openings, and keep the written answer. The questions on imputed rent and insurance go to the ACI and the ECA.

1

Garage turned into a living room: habitable area

For · BTI · ACI · ECA
  1. Does turning the garage (about 28 m² inside, 4.98 × 5.60 m) into a living room count towards any index or limit of the regulation (PEP footprint ratio, PACom ISB), or only the footprint, which does not change?
  2. Is it a change of use that needs a permit? Which habitability rules apply (minimum height 2.40 m, glazing at least 1/8 of the floor)? Is a height of about 2.35–2.40 m after floor insulation accepted?
  3. Does the declared habitable area change anything for the commune (taxes, connections)?
  4. ACI and ECA: by how much do the imputed rent, the tax valuation and the insured value rise?
What we knowThe PEP ratio and the PACom ISB count footprint, not floor area: in principle no capacity is used.Habitable area would grow from about 192 to 220 m². The heated area grows too: the new floor, walls and glazing must meet current energy rules.
2

Two enclosed parking spaces elsewhere on the parcel

For · BTI
  1. If the garage becomes a living room, can we build an enclosed two-car garage (or a carport) elsewhere on the parcel, for instance near today’s access at the top? Where exactly, and how big?
  2. Is it accepted as a minor outbuilding (RLATC art. 39, about 40 m²) within the boundary distances, or up to the boundary with the neighbour’s written consent (PACom art. 3.3.4)? What maximum height? Is a flat roof allowed (PACom art. 8.6.2)?
  3. How many spaces does the commune require for this house (VSS norms, PACom art. 5.2)? Are the existing outdoor spaces enough, or is there a compensatory tax without covered spaces?
  4. Can we convert the garage now and build the covered spaces later, with a separate permit?
What we knowUnder both regulations a detached garage of about 40 m² is plausible, but it is a Municipality authorisation, not a right.Limits to keep in mind: at least 50% of open soil (PACom), the road building limit at the south corner, the LLavaux sector.
3

Permits: which ones, and which procedure

For · BTI
  1. One permit for everything (façades, change of use of the garage, interior bearing walls, external insulation, heat pump, solar panels, terrace), or several applications?
  2. Public inquiry or exemption (RLATC art. 72d)? Does the project go to the Commission consultative de Lavaux (LLavaux art. 5a)?
  3. Solar panels: simple notice (LAT art. 18a) or permit, in the LLavaux sector? Air-water heat pump: which noise proof is needed?
  4. Which documents are needed: architect’s plans, asbestos survey (building before 1991), CECB or energy proof, engineer’s opinion? How long should we count?
  5. Can interior work (kitchen, bathrooms, floors) start without a permit while the application is processed?
What we knowDefault track is the public inquiry: about 6–9 months, 12–18 if contested.Subsidies (Programme Bâtiments) must be requested before works start.
4

Openings: can we do them as we like?

For · BTI · Lavaux commission
  1. Are there rules on the number, size or proportions of new façade openings (PEP, PACom art. 3.9 on integration)? In practice, does the commune accept:
    • two French doors down to the floor in the living space (2.85 and 2.9 m), cutting the concrete band’s sill;
    • a French door in the main bedroom;
    • a glass bay in place of the garage door, in the existing opening;
    • glass guards, including in place of the upper terrace’s parapet?
  2. Are there requirements on frame materials and colours, and on reflective glazing (seen from the lake and the vineyards)?
  3. West and south-west façades, 4.0–4.8 m from the boundaries (less than the regulation’s 6 m): can openings be enlarged there within the existing volume (LATC art. 80)?
What we knowNo rule found bans these openings. The risks are the integration clause and the Lavaux commission, which may want the horizontal band kept as a continuous line.Structurally, the band may be load-bearing: probe it before the application.
Orientation questions, not legal advice. Regulations: PEP “Vignoble de Corseaux” (in force), PACom adopted on 02.03.2026 (awaiting cantonal approval), LATC / RLATC, LLavaux.
