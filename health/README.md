# Health App Business Project

This directory contains the health app startup business project, including product specifications, market research, and business planning documents.

## Directory Structure

### `/app/` - Product Pages (18 HTML files)
Contains the health app product interface mockups and feature specifications:
- Core features (dashboard, biomarker tracking, health insights)
- Specialized modules (hormone tracking, metabolic health, supplements)
- User flows and interface designs

### `/business-docs/` - Business Planning
Business plan, project specifications, and strategic documentation for the health app venture.

### `/research/` - Market Research (13 documents)
- Competitor analysis
- Market segmentation studies
- Industry research and trends
- EU health app market analysis

### `/health-app-coach/` and `/health-intelligence-platform/` - Earlier drafts
Two earlier, unmerged drafts of this same business plan (different naming/structuring passes over the concept). Kept as-is alongside the canonical structure above rather than merged in — review by hand before relying on them.

### Root Level Files
- **baby-development-timeline.html** & **-fr.html** - Family-level health tracking (not individual analysis)
- **hormone-performance-apps-analysis.md** - Competitive analysis of hormone and performance apps
- **eu-health-app-market-analysis.html** - European health app market landscape

## Personal Health Analyses

Reusable health analysis frameworks are documented in `/skills/health/`:

- These frameworks support both the business product development and personal health analyses

## Purpose

This is the **business project** directory. It contains:
- ✅ Product specifications and mockups
- ✅ Market research and competitive analysis
- ✅ Business strategy and planning documents

It does NOT contain:
- ❌ Individual biomarker test results (those are in `/personal/health/loic/` and `/personal/health/julien/`)
- ❌ Personal health assessments or recommendations
- ❌ Methodology frameworks (those are in `/skills/health/`)
