Market Gaps We're Targeting

True Home Testing

French competitors (Zoī, Lucis, Aro) all require lab visits. We're the only option with convenient at-home finger-prick testing.

Lowest Entry Price

At €39, we're 80% cheaper than Lucis (€190), 92% cheaper than Aro (€499), and 99% cheaper than Zoī (€3,000).

Fastest Results

72-hour turnaround vs 7 days (Aro) or 5 weeks (Lucis). Only Zoī is faster but requires €3,000 and a Paris trip.

No Appointment Needed

Competitors require scheduling at labs or clinics. We ship to your door - test anytime that works for you.
