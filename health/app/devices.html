Key Takeaways

B2B Pricing Dominates

Most devices use quote-based B2B pricing. Only Tasso+ (~$25-30/unit) and Capitainer B10 (~€7.90/unit) have confirmed public pricing.

Liquid vs. Dried Trade-off

Liquid blood devices (Tasso+, TAP) collect more volume for full biomarker panels. Dried blood spot devices offer cheaper logistics with no cold chain needed.

EU Regulatory Ready

Mitra, HemaXis, TAP Micro, Tasso+, Capitainer, Vitestro Aletta, and Thriva all carry CE marking for the European market.

Strong European Ecosystem

Key EU players include Loop Medical (Switzerland/EPFL), Vitestro (Netherlands), Labonovum (Netherlands), Capitainer (Sweden), HemaXis (Switzerland), Physikit (Germany), and Thriva (UK).

Patient Preference

50% of patients prefer fingerprick over venipuncture. Upper-arm devices (Tasso, TAP, Thriva Autodraw) report even lower pain — 91% of Thriva users say it's pain-free.

Highest Volume Collectors

Loop Medical Onflow collects up to 1.5 mL, TAP Micro Select up to 0.9 mL, and Thriva Autodraw up to 0.8 mL — enough for full biomarker panels.
