Clinical Guidelines Position

ACOG (American College of OB-GYN)

  • Menopause is a clinical diagnosis (12 months no period in woman >45)
  • Routine hormone testing NOT recommended for typical presentation
  • Testing useful for: atypical cases, POI, HRT monitoring

NAMS (North American Menopause Society)

  • Symptom-based approach for diagnosis
  • Testing recommended when: age <45, surgical menopause, HRT optimization
When Testing IS Clinically Useful:
  1. Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI): Women <40 with menopause symptoms
  2. HRT Monitoring: Especially transdermal estrogen users
  3. Differential Diagnosis: Rule out thyroid dysfunction, PCOS
  4. Perimenopausal Uncertainty: Women 40-45 with irregular cycles
  5. Contraception Decisions: When to stop birth control safely

Bone Health

30%

Bone loss accelerates in first 5-7 years. Vitamin D status critical (70% of French women insufficient).

Cardiovascular Risk

+50%

LDL typically rises 10-15%. ApoB may be better predictor than LDL.

Metabolic Changes

+0.1-0.2%

Insulin resistance increases with estrogen decline. Abdominal fat accumulation common.
