Psychographic Profile

Managing an ongoing health condition: thyroid issues, diabetes risk, high cholesterol, hormonal imbalances, anemia. Needs regular monitoring but faces barriers: no GP (6.7M affected), long wait times, inconvenient lab visits.

The segment most underserved by France's healthcare system. Price-sensitive but willing to pay for convenience and frequency. Chronic disease management is the largest wearable market segment at 41% of revenue.

Current Behavior

  • Regular blood tests (prescribed by GP, 2-4x/year)
  • Frustrated by slow lab results and inconvenient appointments
  • May already use connected health devices for monitoring
  • 25.4% of French people forgone healthcare due to cost/access
  • 1 in 3 gave up seeking medical care in 2024

Health Needs

  • Condition-specific monitoring panels (thyroid, metabolic, hormonal)
  • Frequency: monthly or quarterly tracking
  • Trend alerts (when values move outside personal range)
  • Integration with physician records
  • Medication impact tracking

Acquisition Channels

  • Patient associations (Fed. Francaise des Diabetiques, Ligue contre le cancer)
  • Pharmacist recommendations
  • GP referrals (complement to existing care)
  • Health forums (Doctissimo, Sante Magazine)
  • Google: "suivi thyroide a domicile" / "analyse cholesterol sans ordonnance"

Product Requirements

  • Condition-specific panels (customizable)
  • Subscription model with flexible frequency
  • Personal baseline tracking with alerts
  • Easy sharing with healthcare provider
  • Reminders and scheduling automation
  • Integration with ALD documentation

Trust Signals

Position as complement to medical care, not replacement. Emphasize frequency, convenience, affordability. Results compatible with existing medical records.

Competitor Overlap: No direct competitor offers affordable home-based chronic condition monitoring in France. This is a unique market position.
