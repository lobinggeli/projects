Psychographic Profile

Time-poor but health-aware. Buys organic regularly (index above average — millennials with children are top organic buyers). Takes supplements. Interested in family health but lacks time for GP visits. Trusts healthcare professionals over social media. Values peace of mind and convenience.

Current Behavior

  • Visits GP 2-3x/year (for self and children)
  • Gets blood tests when prescribed but doesn't proactively seek them
  • Uses Doctolib for appointments; familiar with digital health tools
  • 44% of ages 40-59 download health apps
  • May have a mutuelle covering family (95% have one)

Health Needs

  • Comprehensive health check (thyroid, hormones, metabolic markers)
  • Energy/fatigue investigation (iron, B12, vitamin D, thyroid)
  • Stress and cortisol markers
  • Cholesterol and cardiovascular risk
  • Tracking over time (biannual testing)
