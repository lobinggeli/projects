Psychographic Profile

Successful career but aware of stress toll. Works 50+ hours/week. Knows they should do more for their health but lacks time. Willing to pay premium for convenience. Values discretion and quality.

Comparable to Zoi's target but priced out at €3,000/year. The "executive wellness" segment that McKinsey's "confident enthusiasts" maps to.
