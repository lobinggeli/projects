Age 45-58 | €30K-80K income | 100% female

Experiencing hormonal changes with real daily impact. 87% report symptoms. Frustrated by a healthcare system that largely ignores menopause. Cultural taboo breaking rapidly with 2025 parliamentary report. 70%+ use supplements.
