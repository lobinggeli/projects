Understanding Sample Testing

How blood is collected, how much is needed, and whether at-home devices can replace a lab visit.

Venous Blood Draw

Gold Standard

A trained phlebotomist draws blood from a vein in your arm using a needle and vacuum tubes. Used by lab-visit providers like Lucis, Aro, Zoi, Function Health.

  • Volume5–40 mL (2–8 tubes)
  • Biomarkers possible200+ (unlimited)
  • AccuracyHighest (reference standard)
  • WhereLab or clinic visit required
  • Who uses thisLucis, Aro, Zoi, Function Health, Aware

Capillary / Finger Prick

At-Home Friendly

A small lancet pricks your fingertip to collect capillary blood into a microtube. Studies show results are clinically equivalent to venous draws for most general chemistry analytes.

  • Volume0.1–0.9 mL
  • Biomarkers possibleUp to 70–80
  • AccuracyEquivalent for most markers*
  • WhereAt home (self-collection)
  • Who uses thisThriva, Forth, Medichecks, SiPhox

Dried Blood Spot (DBS)

Microsampling

Blood drops are collected onto filter paper or absorbent tips, then dried and mailed. Excellent for storage and transport, but supports fewer biomarkers.

  • Volume0.01–0.1 mL (a few drops)
  • Biomarkers possibleUp to 20–40
  • AccuracyGood for targeted markers
  • WhereAt home (mail-in)
  • Who uses thisSome Lykon panels, research studies

Can At-Home Devices Replace a Lab Visit?

Mapping the devices from our devices page to what providers actually need.

Basic Panels (25–50 markers)

Fully viable at home

Panels like Lykon, Medichecks, Biostarks, and basic Forth/Thriva tests need <1 mL of blood. Any at-home device works.

  • DevicesTasso+, TAP, SiPhox EasyDraw, Thriva Autodraw
  • Volume needed0.2–0.8 mL
  • CoversCBC, lipids, metabolic, thyroid, vitamins

Mid-Range Panels (50–80 markers)

Partially viable

Thriva (70+), Forth (70+), and Aware (76) panels push the limits of finger-prick collection. Some advanced markers may require venous draw or multiple collections.

  • DevicesTasso+ (0.6 mL), TAP Micro Select (0.9 mL), Onflow (1.5 mL)
  • Volume needed0.5–1.5 mL
  • LimitationSome hormones and specialized markers need more volume

Comprehensive Panels (100+ markers)

Lab visit required

Lucis Care (110+), Function Health (100+), Zoi (200+), and Superpower (100+) require 15–60 mL of blood across multiple tube types. No at-home device can handle this.

  • Volume needed15–60 mL (multiple tubes)
  • WhyDifferent anticoagulants per test group (EDTA, heparin, serum)
  • Also needsUrine, saliva (Lucis Care), imaging (Zoi)

Dried Blood Spot (DBS) Panels

Limited scope only

Devices like Mitra, Capitainer B, and HemaXis collect micro-volumes onto absorbent material. Great for targeted testing but only supports a subset of biomarkers.

  • DevicesMitra (VAMS), Capitainer B50, HemaXis DB
  • Volume0.01–0.1 mL
  • Best forHbA1c, vitamin D, thyroid, specific hormones
