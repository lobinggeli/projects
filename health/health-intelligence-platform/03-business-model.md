# Business Model

## Overview

Hybrid model combining one-time comprehensive analysis with subscription-based daily coaching, designed to maximize conversion and lifetime value.

---

## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Comprehensive Analysis (One-Time)

**Price**: CHF 149 (€140)

**What's Included**:
- Upload unlimited lab reports (past 2 years of testing)
- Connect all wearables (Oura Ring, Apple Watch, Whoop, CGM)
- Comprehensive health analysis using 17 proprietary frameworks
- Prioritized action plan (supplements, lifestyle changes, further testing)
- Detailed PDF report (20-30 pages)
- Web dashboard with visualizations
- Valid for 90 days (can purchase quarterly re-analysis)

**Target Customer**:
- New users wanting to "try" the platform
- Users who only want occasional deep-dive analysis
- Budget-conscious biohackers

**Expected Volume**: 30% of total customers stay at this tier

**Repeat Purchase Rate**: 70% purchase again within 6 months

---

### Tier 2: Daily AI Coach (Subscription)

**Price**: CHF 79/month (€75) OR CHF 849/year (save 10%)

**Prerequisites**: Must have completed Tier 1 analysis (or within past 6 months)

**What's Included**:
- Everything in Comprehensive Analysis (one-time)
- Daily morning readiness brief (delivered 6-7am)
- Personalized training recommendations based on recovery
- Supplement protocol reminders + progress tracking
- Nutrition guidance (macro targeting, meal timing, CGM-based)
- Recovery optimization (sleep interventions, stress management)
- Weekly progress summary emails
- Monthly trend analysis reports
- Unlimited re-analysis when new labs are uploaded

**Target Customer**:
- Users who loved the initial analysis and want daily guidance
- Active biohackers training 4-6x/week
- Users with specific protocols they want to track

**Expected Volume**: 50% of total customers

**Retention**: 75% at 90 days, 65% at 12 months

---

### Tier 3: Premium (All-Inclusive)

**Price**: CHF 129/month (€120) OR CHF 1,399/year (save 10%)

**What's Included**:
- Everything in Daily AI Coach
- **Quarterly comprehensive re-analysis included** (CHF 149 value × 4 = CHF 596/year savings)
- Priority support (24-hour response time via email/chat)
- Early access to new features (beta access)
- Advanced supplement protocol design (personalized stacks with interaction checking)
- Doctor-sharable reports (professional PDFs formatted for medical review)
- Advanced visualization dashboard (multi-biomarker correlations)
- 1-on-1 onboarding call (30 minutes with health analyst)

**Target Customer**:
- Power users doing quarterly blood work
- Users with complex health situations (multiple protocols)
- Users who value premium support

**Expected Volume**: 20% of total customers

**Retention**: 85% at 90 days, 75% at 12 months

## Why This Model Works

### Low Barrier to Entry
- **CHF 149 one-time** lets users try the analysis without subscription commitment
- Lower psychological barrier than "CHF 79/month" upfront
- Users experience the value before committing to recurring payment

### Clear Upgrade Path
- After seeing comprehensive analysis, natural desire for daily coaching emerges
- "I just learned all these insights - now I want to act on them every day"
- Upsell messaging: "You just spent CHF 149 on analysis... now use it every day for CHF 79/month"

### High Conversion Funnel
Expected conversion rates:
1. **Analysis → Daily Coach**: 60-70% within 3 months
2. **Daily Coach → Premium**: 20-30% within 6 months
3. **Blended**: 50-60% of users on subscriptions within 6 months

### Recurring Revenue Foundation
- Target: 70% of customers on subscriptions (Daily Coach or Premium)
- MRR growth from both new customers + upgrades
- Annual plans improve cash flow + lock in customers

### Premium Positioning
- CHF 129/month is comfortable for Swiss biohackers already spending CHF 150-300/month
- Price anchoring: Premium tier makes Daily Coach seem affordable
- Creates aspirational tier for power users

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Analysis-Only Customers (30% of total):**
- Initial purchase: CHF 149
- Repeat purchases: 70% purchase again within 6 months
- Average purchases over 2 years: 4 analyses
- **LTV**: CHF 149 × 4 = **CHF 596**

**Daily Coach Customers (50% of total):**
- Initial analysis: CHF 149
- Subscription: CHF 79/month
- Average subscription duration: 18 months
- **LTV**: CHF 149 + (CHF 79 × 18) = **CHF 1,571**

**Premium Customers (20% of total):**
- Subscription: CHF 129/month
- Average subscription duration: 24 months (higher retention)
- **LTV**: CHF 129 × 24 = **CHF 3,096**

**Blended LTV**:
- (0.30 × CHF 596) + (0.50 × CHF 1,571) + (0.20 × CHF 3,096)
- = CHF 179 + CHF 786 + CHF 619
- = **CHF 1,584** (~**CHF 1,650** with upsells)

## Pricing Strategy Rationale

### Why CHF 149 for Analysis?

**Competitive Comparison:**
- InsideTracker: $129-589 per test (no daily coaching)
- Biostarks: CHF 200-800 per panel (lab only, no interpretation)
- Functional medicine consult: CHF 300-500 (one-time)

**Our Position**: CHF 149 is **affordable enough** to try but **premium enough** to signal quality

**Anchoring Effect**: Makes CHF 79/month subscription seem like great value

---

### Why CHF 79 for Daily Coach?

**Competitive Comparison:**
- Oura subscription: CHF 6/month (basic insights only)
- Whoop subscription: CHF 30/month (basic insights only)
- MacroFactor: CHF 15/month (nutrition only)
- Human health coach: CHF 200-500/session

**Our Position**: CHF 79 = **3x Whoop price** but **10x more value** (lab + wearable + AI coaching)

**Value Perception**: "I'm already paying CHF 30 for Whoop + CHF 149 for quarterly analysis... CHF 79 to connect them makes sense"

---

### Why CHF 129 for Premium?

**Value Math:**
- 4 quarterly analyses: CHF 596/year (CHF 149 × 4)
- Daily Coach: CHF 948/year (CHF 79 × 12)
- **Total**: CHF 1,544/year if purchased separately

**Premium Tier**: CHF 1,548/year (same price) but with:
- Priority support
- Advanced features
- Early access to new features

**Positioning**: For power users who want "best-in-class" experience

## Churn Reduction Strategy

### High-Risk Churn Indicators

**Red Flags:**
- No wearable connection after 7 days
- Daily Coach emails unopened for 7+ days in a row
- No new lab upload within 6 months
- Low engagement with protocols (no check-ins)

**Intervention:**
- Automated email: "We noticed you haven't been using [feature] - need help?"
- Offer 1-on-1 onboarding call (15 min)
- Discount offer: "Stay another 3 months, get 20% off"

---

### Retention Tactics

**Month 1-3: Habit Formation**
- Weekly check-in emails
- Tutorial content (how to maximize platform value)
- Quick wins (show early improvements)

**Month 3-6: Protocol Completion**
- Celebrate milestones ("30 days of magnesium protocol complete!")
- Show measurable improvements ("HRV +12% since starting")
- Retest reminders

**Month 6+: Community & Long-Term Value**
- Share user success stories
- Introduce new features (keeps platform fresh)
- Community forum (users connect with each other)
