### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**By Customer Segment:**

**Analysis-Only Customers (30% of base):**
- Initial purchase: CHF 149
- Repeat purchases: 3.5 analyses over 2 years
- LTV: CHF 149 × 3.5 = **CHF 522**
- Gross margin LTV: CHF 522 × 95% = **CHF 496**

**Daily Coach Customers (50% of base):**
- Initial analysis: CHF 149
- Subscription: CHF 79 × 18 months = CHF 1,422
- LTV: CHF 149 + CHF 1,422 = **CHF 1,571**
- Gross margin LTV: CHF 1,571 × 96% = **CHF 1,508**

**Premium Customers (20% of base):**
- Subscription: CHF 129 × 24 months = CHF 3,096
- LTV: **CHF 3,096**
- Gross margin LTV: CHF 3,096 × 94% = **CHF 2,910**

**Blended LTV:**
- (0.30 × CHF 522) + (0.50 × CHF 1,571) + (0.20 × CHF 3,096)
- = CHF 157 + CHF 786 + CHF 619
- = **CHF 1,562**

**Gross Margin Blended LTV: CHF 1,500**

**Year 1:**
- LTV: CHF 1,562

- Strong subscription conversion (60-70%)
- Long subscription durations (18-24 months)
- Low churn for engaged users

## Conclusion

The health intelligence platform demonstrates exceptional unit economics with:

1. **Fast Path to Profitability** - Profitable from Month 8
2. **High Margins** - 43% EBITDA in Year 1, 74% in Year 2-3
3. **Strong Unit Economics** - LTV:CAC of 17.5:1
4. **Capital Efficient** - Can bootstrap with CHF 25-40K initial investment
5. **Scalable Model** - Low variable costs enable high-margin growth
6. **Multiple Revenue Streams** - Diversified between one-time and recurring

The financial model is conservative and includes realistic churn assumptions. Even in downside scenarios (50% lower acquisition), the business remains profitable within 12 months.
