# Success Metrics & KPIs

## Overview

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) and success metrics for the health intelligence platform across all phases of development and growth.

---

## North Star Metric

**Active Daily Coach Subscribers**

**Definition**: Number of paying subscribers actively using the Daily Coach or Premium tier with at least one wearable connected and daily engagement.

**Why This Metric**:
- Captures both revenue (subscription) and engagement (active usage)
- Correlates directly with long-term business value and retention
- Indicates product-market fit (users paying AND using daily)
- Drives all other metrics (MRR, retention, LTV)

**Target Growth**:
- Month 6: 40-50 active subscribers
- Month 12: 280-360 active subscribers
- Year 2: 675-750 active subscribers
- Year 3: 1,450-1,500 active subscribers

## Target Setting Methodology

### OKR Framework (Quarterly)

**Example Q1 OKRs:**

**Objective 1**: Validate Product-Market Fit
- KR1: Achieve 50 paying users by end of Q1
- KR2: Deliver 95%+ of analysis reports within 48 hours
- KR3: Achieve NPS of 50+ from first 30 users

**Objective 2**: Build Daily Coach Foundation
- KR1: Launch Daily Coach MVP by Month 4
- KR2: Achieve 60% conversion from analysis to subscription trial
- KR3: Maintain 85%+ email open rate for daily briefs

**Objective 3**: Establish Growth Channels
- KR1: Publish 12 SEO-optimized blog posts
- KR2: Achieve <CHF 100 blended CAC
- KR3: Generate 30% of signups from organic/referral sources

---

### Leading vs. Lagging Indicators

**Leading Indicators (Predict Future Performance):**
- Daily email open rates → Future engagement & retention
- Wearable connection rate → Subscription conversion likelihood
- Time to first action → Long-term engagement
- Content traffic growth → Future CAC reduction
- Trial signup rate → Future MRR growth

**Lagging Indicators (Measure Past Performance):**
- MRR & ARR
- LTV:CAC ratio
- Monthly churn rate
- NPS scores
- Revenue per user

**Focus**: Track leading indicators daily/weekly to drive lagging indicator improvements.
