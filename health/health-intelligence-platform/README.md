# Health Intelligence Platform - Business Plan

**Last Updated**: February 9, 2026

## Executive Summary

A health intelligence platform that aggregates lab data + wearable data to provide comprehensive analysis and daily AI-powered recommendations. Focused on the Swiss biohacker market.

**Core Value Proposition**: "Your AI health analyst that connects the dots between your lab work and daily recovery data to give you personalized recommendations every morning"

## Strategic Pivot Context

**What Changed from Original Super App Concept:**
- ❌ **Not** a multi-domain super app (no finance, career coaching)
- ❌ **Not** building testing infrastructure (leverage existing labs)
- ❌ **Not** human coaching (pure AI-driven)
- ✅ **Focus**: Lab + wearable data aggregation → AI analysis → daily recommendations
- ✅ **Market**: Switzerland (biohackers/health optimizers)
- ✅ **MVP**: Manual lab upload, no dependencies on lab partnerships

---

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Swiss Market Focus** - First to deeply serve Swiss biohackers
2. **17 Proprietary Health Frameworks** - Deeper insights than generic analysis
3. **Wearable-First Approach** - Daily coaching vs. quarterly reports
4. **No Lab Dependencies** - Works with ANY lab report
5. **Premium Positioning** - Sustainable margins in high-value market
