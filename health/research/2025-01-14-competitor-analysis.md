## Market Gaps & Opportunities

### Underserved Areas
1. **France** - No major player targeting French market
2. **Price-conscious consumers** - All competitors premium-priced
3. **Advanced AI** - All have basic insights only
4. **True pan-European** - Most are single-country focused

### Competitive Advantages to Build
1. **30-40% lower pricing** via Eastern European lab partnerships
2. **Superior AI insights** using GPT-4/Claude for personalization
3. **France-first strategy** in underserved market
4. **Home testing + AI** combination (Aware has lab visits, others have basic AI)

### Technical Opportunities
1. Wearable integration (Apple Watch, Oura, Whoop)
2. Multi-language support (French, German, Dutch)
3. Predictive health analytics
4. Comparison with population averages

---

## Recommendations

1. **Pricing**: Position at €35-89 range (30-40% below Thriva/Lykon)
2. **Biomarkers**: Start with 20-30 core biomarkers, expand to 50+
3. **AI**: Invest heavily in personalized insights (key differentiator)
4. **Market**: Launch in France, expand to Belgium/Switzerland
5. **Sampling**: Home finger-prick kits (avoid lab visit model)
6. **Labs**: Partner with Polish/Czech ISO-certified labs
