|----------|---------|

| **Biomarkers** | 100-110+ across 11 health categories |

### Pricing

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Discovery | €190/year (€16/mo) | 60+ biomarkers, single checkup |
| Care | €490/year (€41/mo) | 100+ biomarkers, 2 tests/year, wearable integration |

### Add-ons
- Gut microbiome test: €150
- Food intolerance panel (50-100 foods): €200

### Biomarker Categories (11 systems)
1. Cardiovascular health
2. Metabolic function
3. Renal & hydration
4. Inflammation & immunity
5. Nervous system & recovery
6. Hepatic & digestive
7. Hematology & iron
8. Musculoskeletal
9. Thyroid
10. Sex hormones & reproduction
11. Biological age calculation

### How It Works
1. Book lab test at one of 400+ partner labs (15-20 min)
2. Receive results within ~5 weeks
3. Get personalized guidance on nutrition, exercise, sleep, mindset, environment
4. Follow-up test at 6-month mark to track progress

### Strengths
- Y Combinator backed (credibility)
- Large lab network across 4 European countries
- Affordable pricing (€190-490/year)
- Wearable integrations (Garmin, Strava, Withings, Oura)
- Biological age calculation
- No prescription required
- Comprehensive biomarker coverage

### Weaknesses
- Very new company (founded 2025)
- Requires lab visit (not home testing)
- Long turnaround time (~5 weeks)
- No doctor consultation included
- No physical presence/center

### Key Differentiators
- "Function Health for Europe" positioning
- AI-powered wellness coaching
- Educational content included
- Private dashboard for tracking
- Medical team review of results

## Strategic Implications

### Lucis Threat Level: Medium-High
- Direct competitor in affordable segment
- Strong backing (YC, General Catalyst)
- Pan-European expansion underway
- Similar price point to our mid-tier

### Aro Threat Level: Low-Medium
- France-only focus
- Smaller scale operation
- Less comprehensive offering
- No significant differentiator

### Our Competitive Response
1. **Home testing advantage** - Neither Lucis nor Aro offer home collection
2. **Faster results** - 72h vs 5 weeks (Lucis) or 7 days (Aro)
3. **Lower entry price** - €39 vs €190 (Lucis) or €499 (Aro)
4. **Convenience** - No lab visits required
