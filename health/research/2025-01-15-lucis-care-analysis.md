# Competitor Analysis: Lucis (France) & Care (Switzerland)

**Date:** January 15, 2025

---

## Lucis (France)

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| HQ | Paris, France |
| Founded | 2025 |
| Funding | $8.5M Seed (General Catalyst, Y Combinator, Kima Ventures) |
| Founders | Maxime Berthelot, Baptiste Debever, Max Guerois |
| Biomarkers | 60-100+ depending on plan |
| Pricing | €190-€490/year |
| Turnaround | ~5 weeks |
| Platforms | Web dashboard, wearable integrations |
| Markets | France, UK, Ireland, Portugal |
| Lab Network | 400+ labs |
| Rating | Mixed (Trustpilot: 6 reviews, some negative feedback on onboarding) |

### Pricing Plans

| Plan | Price | Biomarkers | Includes |
|------|-------|------------|----------|
| **Discovery** | €190/year (€16/mo) | 60+ | 1 checkup, dashboard, action plan |
| **Care** | €490/year (€41/mo) | 100+ | 2 checkups/year, wearables, personalized actions |

**Add-ons:**
- Gut Microbiome Test: €150
- Food Intolerances Panel: €200

### Strengths
- Very competitive pricing (€190-490 vs €500-2000 for comparable tests)
- Strong backing (YC, General Catalyst)
- AI-powered health scores and personalized recommendations
- Large lab network across multiple EU countries
- GDPR compliant, HDS-certified

### Weaknesses
- Early stage (only 3,000 customers)
- Buggy onboarding process reported by users
- Slow turnaround (~5 weeks for results)
- No dedicated mobile app found
- Mixed reviews on communication and appointment booking

### Key Differentiators
- Positioned as "Function Health for Europe"
- Focus on making preventive care affordable and accessible
- 15 health scores + biological age tracking
- Wearable integrations (Garmin, Strava, Withings)

## Strategic Implications for Our App

### Pricing Opportunity
- Lucis at €190-490/year sets aggressive pricing in France
- Our 30-40% lower positioning would mean €115-295/year
- Care's CHF 690+ shows Swiss market tolerates premium pricing

### Feature Gaps to Exploit
1. **Mobile-first experience** - Lucis lacks dedicated app, Care is iOS only
2. **Faster turnaround** - Lucis's 5-week wait is a pain point
3. **Better onboarding** - Lucis users complain about buggy process
4. **Cross-platform** - Neither has strong Android presence

### Market Entry Considerations
- France: Compete directly with Lucis on price + better UX
- Switzerland: Premium market, insurance integration is key differentiator
- Both markets value medical certification and data security
