### Strengths
- Most comprehensive assessment in France (200 biomarkers + 38 assessments)
- Premium luxury experience at Vendôme center
- Advanced imaging included (DEXA, ultrasounds, cardiac)
- Strong investor backing (Moderna CEO, Xavier Niel)
- 12-month personalized follow-up via app
- Life Journal with daily actionable recommendations
- High-profile co-founders with political connections
- No waiting room - private suite experience

### Weaknesses
- Very high price point (€3,000/year)
- Paris only - no other locations
- Requires in-person visit (not home testing)
- Not covered by French healthcare (Sécurité Sociale)
- New app with limited user reviews
- No Android app found
- Limited scalability (premium model)
- 4-hour time commitment required

### Key Differentiators
- Ultra-premium positioning in French market
- Combines medical checkup with ongoing digital coaching
- Focus on longevity and preventive medicine
- Personalized "Life Journal" for habit formation
- Exclusive investor network (Moderna, Iliad, CMA-CGM)
- Former Macron advisor as co-founder (media attention)

## Competitive Implications

### Market Position
Zoī targets the ultra-premium segment (€3,000/year) with an in-person luxury experience. This leaves significant opportunity for:
1. **Mid-market pricing** - Our €39-159 range is 95% cheaper
2. **Home testing convenience** - No need to visit Paris
3. **Broader geographic reach** - Serve all of France, not just Paris

### Pricing Comparison

| Provider | Entry Price | Premium Price | Home Testing |
|----------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| Zoī | €3,000 | €3,000 | No |
| **Us** | €39 | €159 | Yes |
| Thriva | ~€82 | ~€175 | Yes |
| Lykon | €89 | €149 | Yes |

### Strategic Takeaways
1. Zoī validates the French market appetite for preventive health
2. Their premium pricing creates room for affordable alternatives
3. Home testing + AI could deliver similar value at 1/20th the cost
4. Their €23M funding shows investor interest in French preventive health
