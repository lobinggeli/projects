## 5. Key Takeaways for Expansion Strategy

1. **Germany is the priority expansion market** — 84M population, largest fitness/wellness market in EU, established DTC blood testing acceptance, DiGA regulatory pathway for potential reimbursement
2. **Switzerland offers highest revenue per user** — €2,560 OOP/yr means Swiss customers self-pay for health without friction; French-speaking 23% is a direct linguistic bridge
3. **Belgium is the lowest-friction entry** — French-speaking 40%, strong eHealth infrastructure, mHealth reimbursement pathway, geographic proximity
4. **Total EU-4 addressable market is ~65M** — roughly 2.6x the French market alone
5. **Key risk: Germany has existing DTC competitors** (Cerascreen, Lykon, Baze) — differentiation through AI insights and pricing advantage is critical
