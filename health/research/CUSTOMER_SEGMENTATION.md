# Customer Segmentation - Health App Biomarker Platform

**Date:** January 27, 2026
**Market:** France (primary), Belgium, Switzerland, Germany (secondary)

---

## 1. Methodology

### Framework
Four-dimensional segmentation: demographic, psychographic, behavioral, and needs-based. Segments validated against competitor customer data and French market research.

### Data Sources
- OECD Health at a Glance 2025 (France Country Profile)
- DREES Health Expenditure Reports (2024 edition)
- BEUC Consumer Health Data Sharing Survey (2023, N=8,067 across 8 EU countries including France)
- McKinsey Future of Wellness (2024-2025)
- Philips Future Health Index 2025 (16 countries including France)
- Innova Market Insights: Health & Wellness Trends in France
- European Journal of Public Health: mHealth adoption (2025)
- Nature/Scientific Reports: AI hesitancy in France (2025)
- Grand View Research: France Biohacking Market, Anti-Aging Market
- Statista: Digital Health France, Fitness Industry, Organic Food
- NutriNet-Santé Cohort studies (PMC)
- Santé Publique France: Cancer screening participation (2024)
- Competitor analysis: Lucis, Zoī, Thriva, Lykon, Function Health (internal research files)

### Limitations
- France-specific willingness-to-pay data for biomarker testing is limited; global/European proxies used where needed
- Competitor customer demographics are estimated from public sources (reviews, press, investor materials)
- Some market size figures use USD/EUR conversions at approximate rates

### Consumer Health Behaviors

- **78%** have taken action to live more healthily in the past 12 months
- **67%** take at least one supplement; vitamins are #1
- **46%** actively limit sugar intake
- **40%** of adults are overweight; **26%** are obese
- **25.4%** have forgone healthcare due to cost (national survey, N=164,092)
- **42%** have teleconsulted at least once (95.4% satisfaction)
- **44%** breast cancer screening participation (target: 70%)
- **28%** colorectal cancer screening participation (target: 45%)

### Key Market Dynamics

**Demand drivers:**
- Prevention spending gap (2.3% vs. 3.4% OECD avg) = underserved market
- 6.7M people without a GP = bottleneck for prescription-based blood tests
- Post-COVID normalization of self-testing
- Growing biohacking/longevity community (France: USD 1.09B market, 18.4% CAGR)
- DTC lab testing growing at 8.94% CAGR globally

**Barriers:**
- Only 8% trust wellness apps with health data (vs. 88% trust their GP)
- French consumers have the **lowest trust in online companies** among surveyed EU countries
- DTC genetic testing is illegal in France (EUR 3,750 fine)
- Strong cultural expectation that health services should be reimbursed
- Subscription fatigue: France has fewest subscriptions in Europe (3.0 avg)

The cultural taboo is breaking rapidly — a 2025 French parliamentary report proposed 25 measures, and HAS is updating menopause guidelines (first update since the 1970s) expected in 2026. Already spending on supplements: 70%+ of menopausal women in Western Europe use dietary supplements. Looking for evidence-based tools in a market flooded with unvalidated apps (236 menopause apps exist globally, fewer than a third are evidence-based).

**Current behavior:**
- Visits GP 2-4x/year but often does not raise menopause symptoms
- May not have easy access to a gynecologist (shortage in rural France)

- Parenting/women's platforms (Femme Actuelle, Santé Magazine, Doctissimo forums)
- Google: "test hormones ménopause" / "bilan hormonal périménopause" / "ménopause analyse sang"
