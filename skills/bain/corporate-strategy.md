# Bain Corporate Strategy Framework

Use this framework to develop comprehensive corporate strategy. It combines five essential elements that together create a coherent strategic plan.

## The Five Elements

### 1. Bold Yet Realistic Ambition
**Define where you want to go**

- Set aspirational but achievable goals
- Balance stretch targets with feasibility
- Anchor in market reality and capabilities
- Time-bound with clear milestones

**Key Questions:**
- What does winning look like for us?
- What market position do we aspire to?
- What financial targets are we pursuing?

### 2. Portfolio of Assets
**Determine what businesses to own**

- Evaluate each business unit's strategic fit
- Assess synergies between units
- Identify gaps requiring M&A
- Determine divestiture candidates

**Key Questions:**
- Which businesses are core vs. non-core?
- Where do we have the right to win?
- What should we acquire, grow, or divest?

### 3. Smart Financial Choices
**Allocate capital effectively**

- Prioritize investments by strategic value
- Balance growth investment vs. returns
- Optimize capital structure
- Manage risk across the portfolio

**Key Questions:**
- How should capital be allocated across units?
- What is our investment thesis for each business?
- How do we balance short-term and long-term?

### 4. Parenting Advantage
**Understand how corporate adds value**

Corporate must add more value than alternative owners through:
- **Standalone influence**: Improving individual businesses
- **Linkage influence**: Creating synergies between businesses
- **Central functions**: Providing shared services efficiently

**Key Questions:**
- Why are these businesses worth more under our ownership?
- What unique capabilities does corporate provide?
- How do we create value beyond what a PE firm could?

### 5. Transformation Roadmap
**Specify actions to get there**

- Sequence strategic initiatives
- Define critical decisions and timing
- Assign accountability
- Establish governance and milestones

**Key Questions:**
- What must change and in what order?
- Who is accountable for each initiative?
- How will we track progress?

## When to Apply
- Annual strategic planning
- CEO/Board strategy sessions
- Post-merger integration planning
- Turnaround situations
- Private equity portfolio strategy

| Source | Description | Example |

| Strategic guidance | Setting direction and priorities | Capital allocation decisions |
| Performance management | Driving accountability | Rigorous operating reviews |
| Talent development | Building leadership pipeline | Cross-unit rotations |
| Shared services | Efficient central functions | IT, Finance, HR |
| Synergy capture | Connecting business units | Shared customers, cross-selling |
| External relationships | Leveraging corporate scale | Supplier negotiations |
