# Bain Five Beliefs on Strategy

Use this framework to guide strategic growth decisions. Based on Bain research into what distinguishes sustained value creators from others.

## The Five Beliefs

### Belief 1: Leadership in Core Business
**"Sustained value creators are overwhelmingly leaders in at least one core business"**

- Market leadership provides advantages in scale, pricing power, and talent attraction
- Focus on becoming #1 or #2 in your core market
- Understand deeply what drives success in your core

### Belief 2: Growth from Core and Adjacencies
**"Most growth comes from the core or one-step adjacencies"**

- Expand from positions of strength
- Adjacent moves leverage existing capabilities
- One-step adjacencies include:
  - New customer segments (similar to current)
  - New geographies (with existing products)
  - New products (for existing customers)
  - New channels (for existing offerings)
  - Value chain expansion (upstream/downstream)

### Belief 3: Adjacency Discipline
**"Most adjacency moves fail—discipline is key"**

- Success rate for adjacencies: ~25%
- Stay close to the core to improve odds
- Each step away from core reduces success probability
- Test adjacencies before full commitment

### Belief 4: Repeatable Model for Expansion
**"Successful companies evolve through one-step moves using a repeatable model"**

- Develop a formula that works and repeat it
- Codify what makes your expansion successful
- Build organizational capabilities around the model
- Consistency beats constant reinvention

### Belief 5: Repeatable Models Double Success
**"Using a repeatable model for expansion can double a company's success rate"**

- ~50% success rate with repeatable model vs ~25% without
- Learning compounds over time
- Reduces execution risk
- Accelerates speed of expansion

## When to Apply
- Growth strategy development
- M&A evaluation
- Market expansion decisions
- Investment prioritization
- Strategic planning sessions

## Core Adjacency Assessment

### Types of Adjacencies (One Step from Core)
| Adjacency Type | Description | Risk Level |
|----------------|-------------|------------|
| Customer segment | Similar customers, same products | Lower |
| Geographic | New region, same products/customers | Medium |
| Product/Service | New offerings, existing customers | Medium |
| Channel | New distribution, same products | Medium |
| Value chain | Vertical integration | Higher |

### Multi-Step Warning
Each additional step away from core increases risk exponentially:
- 1 step: Manageable risk
- 2 steps: Significantly higher risk
- 3+ steps: Usually fails
