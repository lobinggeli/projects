# BCG Growth-Share Matrix

Use this framework to analyze product/business portfolios and guide resource allocation based on market growth rate and relative market share.

## The Four Quadrants

```
                    Relative Market Share
                    High            Low
              ┌─────────────┬─────────────┐
        High  │    STARS    │  QUESTION   │
Market        │      ★      │   MARKS ?   │
Growth        ├─────────────┼─────────────┤
Rate    Low   │    CASH     │    DOGS     │
              │    COWS     │             │
              └─────────────┴─────────────┘
```

### Stars (High Growth, High Share)
- **Characteristics**: Market leaders in fast-growing markets
- **Cash Flow**: High generation, high requirements (net neutral)
- **Strategy**: Invest to maintain leadership, will become Cash Cows
- **Priority**: High investment

### Cash Cows (Low Growth, High Share)
- **Characteristics**: Market leaders in mature markets
- **Cash Flow**: High generation, low requirements (net positive)
- **Strategy**: Milk profits, minimize investment, fund other quadrants
- **Priority**: Harvest, defend position

### Question Marks (High Growth, Low Share)
- **Characteristics**: Weak position in attractive markets
- **Cash Flow**: Low generation, high requirements (net negative)
- **Strategy**: Invest selectively to become Stars OR divest
- **Priority**: Decide: invest heavily or exit

### Dogs (Low Growth, Low Share)
- **Characteristics**: Weak position in unattractive markets
- **Cash Flow**: Low generation, low requirements (break-even or negative)
- **Strategy**: Divest, liquidate, or reposition
- **Priority**: Exit or minimize investment

## When to Apply
- Portfolio management decisions
- Resource allocation planning
- Product line evaluation
- M&A strategy
- Business unit prioritization
- Investment committee reviews

## Key Metrics

### Market Growth Rate
- **High**: >10% annual growth (varies by industry)
- **Low**: <10% annual growth
- Industry average often used as dividing line

### Relative Market Share
- **Formula**: Your market share / Largest competitor's share
- **High**: >1.0 (you are the leader)
- **Low**: <1.0 (competitor is larger)

## Portfolio Strategy

### Ideal Portfolio Balance
- Enough **Cash Cows** to fund investments
- Enough **Stars** for future cash generation
- Selected **Question Marks** as future Stars
- Minimal **Dogs** (only if strategic reasons)

### Cash Flow Dynamics
```
Cash Cows → Fund → Stars (maintain) + Question Marks (build)
Stars → Eventually become → Cash Cows
Question Marks → With investment → Stars OR → Divest
Dogs → Divest → Release resources
```

## Limitations to Consider
- Market definition affects positioning
- Doesn't account for synergies between units
- Market share isn't the only competitive advantage
- Assumes cash flow correlates with position
