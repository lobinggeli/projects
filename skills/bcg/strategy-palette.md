# BCG Strategy Palette

Use this framework to match your strategic approach to your business environment. Different environments require different strategies—one size does not fit all.

## The Five Strategic Environments

BCG identifies strategy based on two dimensions:
- **Predictability**: Can you forecast demand, performance, and competitive dynamics?
- **Malleability**: Can you or others reshape the industry?

```
                        Malleability
                    Low              High
              ┌─────────────┬─────────────┐
Predict-High  │  CLASSICAL  │  VISIONARY  │
ability       ├─────────────┼─────────────┤
        Low   │  ADAPTIVE   │   SHAPING   │
              └─────────────┴─────────────┘

              + RENEWAL (when resources are severely constrained)
```

## The Five Approaches

### 1. Classical Strategy
**Environment**: Predictable, not malleable
**Motto**: "Be big"

- **Approach**: Analyze, plan, execute
- **Goal**: Achieve scale and positional advantage
- **Time Horizon**: 3-5 years
- **Planning Cycle**: Annual
- **Industries**: Oil & gas, utilities, consumer packaged goods

**Key Actions:**
- Rigorous analysis of industry structure
- Clear positioning choices
- Disciplined execution
- Optimize existing business model

### 2. Adaptive Strategy
**Environment**: Unpredictable, not malleable
**Motto**: "Be fast"

- **Approach**: Vary, select, scale
- **Goal**: Flexibility and speed of learning
- **Time Horizon**: <1 year
- **Planning Cycle**: Continuous
- **Industries**: Fashion, technology hardware, specialty retail

**Key Actions:**
- Experiment constantly
- Read and react quickly
- Flexible resource allocation
- Decentralized decision-making

### 3. Visionary Strategy
**Environment**: Predictable, malleable
**Motto**: "Be first"

- **Approach**: Envision, build, persist
- **Goal**: Create or recreate an industry
- **Time Horizon**: 5-10 years
- **Planning Cycle**: Event-driven
- **Industries**: Early-stage pharma, new market creation

**Key Actions:**
- Develop compelling vision
- Commit resources early
- Persist through obstacles
- Build the ecosystem

### 4. Shaping Strategy
**Environment**: Unpredictable, malleable
**Motto**: "Be the orchestrator"

- **Approach**: Engage, orchestrate, evolve
- **Goal**: Shape unpredictable industry to your advantage
- **Time Horizon**: Variable
- **Planning Cycle**: Continuous
- **Industries**: Internet platforms, emerging markets, tech ecosystems

**Key Actions:**
- Build and coordinate platforms/ecosystems
- Engage stakeholders
- Share risk
- Evolve with the ecosystem

### 5. Renewal Strategy
**Environment**: Harsh (resources severely constrained)
**Motto**: "Be viable"

- **Approach**: React, economize, grow
- **Goal**: Restore viability, then grow
- **Time Horizon**: Immediate, then extend
- **Planning Cycle**: Crisis-driven
- **Trigger**: Performance crisis, disruption

**Key Actions:**
- Acknowledge reality
- Economize aggressively
- Pivot business model
- Shift to growth strategy when viable

## When to Apply
- Strategy development and refresh
- Business unit strategy differentiation
- Startup strategy selection
- Crisis response planning
- M&A integration approach

## Environment Assessment

### Predictability Indicators
| Factor | High Predictability | Low Predictability |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Demand patterns | Stable, forecastable | Volatile, uncertain |
| Competitive dynamics | Established players | New entrants, disruption |
| Technology | Mature, slow-changing | Rapid evolution |
| Regulation | Stable framework | Shifting rules |

### Malleability Indicators
| Factor | Low Malleability | High Malleability |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| Industry structure | Consolidated, stable | Fragmented, emerging |
| Barriers to entry | High | Low |
| Customer behavior | Entrenched | Evolving |
| Ecosystem | Fixed | Forming |
