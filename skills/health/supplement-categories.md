# VITAMINS

## Vitamin D3 (Cholecalciferol)

### What It Is
Fat-soluble vitamin essential for calcium absorption, bone health, immune function, mood, and cellular processes. Primarily synthesized in skin via sun exposure; supplementation needed for most people.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Limited sun exposure, dark skin, northern latitudes, age >50, obesity
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue, frequent infections, bone pain, depression, muscle weakness
- **Testing**: 25-OH Vitamin D <30 ng/mL (deficient), 30-50 ng/mL (insufficient)

### Best Form
- **Vitamin D3** (cholecalciferol) >> **Vitamin D2** (ergocalciferol)
- D3 is 2-3x more effective than D2 at raising blood levels
- **Oil-based softgels** > tablets for better absorption
- **Combined with K2** (MK-7) for optimal calcium utilization

### Dosing
| Blood Level (25-OH D) | Therapeutic Dose | Maintenance Dose | Upper Limit |
|-----------------------|------------------|------------------|-------------|
| <20 ng/mL | 5,000-10,000 IU daily | 1,000-2,000 IU | 10,000 IU daily |
| 20-30 ng/mL | 2,000-5,000 IU daily | 1,000-2,000 IU | 10,000 IU daily |
| 30-50 ng/mL | 1,000-2,000 IU daily | 1,000-2,000 IU | 10,000 IU daily |
| 50-80 ng/mL (Optimal) | Maintenance | 1,000-2,000 IU | N/A |

**K2 Pairing**: 100-200 mcg K2 per 5,000 IU D3

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Morning with breakfast
- **With**: Meal containing fat (eggs, avocado, nuts, olive oil)
- **Synergy**: Take with K2 and magnesium (D needs Mg for activation)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Beneficial**: Magnesium, Calcium, K2
- **Caution**: High doses with kidney disease (hypercalcemia risk)
- **Monitor**: Calcium levels if >10,000 IU daily long-term

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Immune**: 6-12 weeks (fewer infections)
- **Mood**: 6-12 weeks (depression reduction)
- **Energy**: 6-12 weeks
- **Bone**: 12+ months (fracture risk reduction)

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Extensive research on bone health, immune function, mood, cardiovascular health

### Cost
$10-20/month

## B-Complex (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9, B12)

### What It Is
Group of 8 water-soluble vitamins essential for energy metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, DNA synthesis, and cellular function.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Chronic stress, poor diet, alcohol consumption, age >50, pregnancy, certain medications (PPIs, metformin)
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue, brain fog, mood changes, numbness/tingling (B12), cracks at mouth corners (B2)

### Best Form
- **Methylated B vitamins** (methylcobalamin, methylfolate) > Synthetic forms (cyanocobalamin, folic acid)
- Especially important if MTHFR genetic mutation (40-60% of population)
- **B-50** or **B-100** complex (numbers refer to mg of major B vitamins)

### Dosing
- **General**: B-50 complex (50 mg major B vitamins) daily
- **Therapeutic**: B-100 complex daily (high stress, deficiency)
- **Maintenance**: B-50 complex or quality multivitamin

**Individual B Vitamins**:
- **B1 (Thiamine)**: 50-100 mg
- **B2 (Riboflavin)**: 50-100 mg
- **B3 (Niacin)**: 50-100 mg (flush-free form to avoid "niacin flush")
- **B5 (Pantothenic acid)**: 50-100 mg
- **B6 (Pyridoxine/P5P)**: 50-100 mg (>100 mg long-term can cause neuropathy)
- **B7 (Biotin)**: 300-1,000 mcg
- **B9 (Folate)**: 400-800 mcg (as methylfolate/5-MTHF)
- **B12 (Cobalamin)**: 500-1,000 mcg (as methylcobalamin)

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Morning with breakfast
- **Why**: Energizing; can interfere with sleep if taken evening
- **Note**: Turns urine bright yellow (riboflavin—normal and harmless)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Generally safe**: Water-soluble (excess excreted)
- **Caution**: B6 >100 mg long-term → neuropathy risk
- **Note**: Biotin >5,000 mcg can interfere with thyroid and cardiac lab tests (stop 3 days before testing)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Energy**: 1-2 weeks
- **Mood**: 2-4 weeks
- **Stress resilience**: 2-4 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Well-established for energy, stress, mood, neurological function

### Cost
$10-25/month

## Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)

### What It Is
Water-soluble vitamin; powerful antioxidant essential for immune function, collagen synthesis, iron absorption, wound healing.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Smokers (2x needs), poor diet, chronic stress, frequent infections
- **Symptoms**: Easy bruising, bleeding gums, slow wound healing, frequent infections
- **Severe Deficiency**: Scurvy (rare in developed countries)

### Best Form
- **Ascorbic acid**: Standard, effective, inexpensive
- **Buffered vitamin C** (calcium/sodium ascorbate): Gentler on stomach
- **Liposomal vitamin C**: Higher absorption, more expensive
- **Ester-C**: Gentler, claims longer retention (mixed evidence)

### Dosing
- **General health**: 500-1,000 mg daily
- **Immune support**: 1,000-2,000 mg daily (divided doses)
- **Acute illness**: 2,000-3,000 mg daily (split into 3 doses)
- **Upper Limit**: 2,000 mg daily (higher amounts cause diarrhea—"bowel tolerance")

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Anytime, with or without food
- **Split doses**: Better absorbed in divided doses (e.g., 500 mg 2x daily vs. 1,000 mg once)
- **With iron**: Enhances iron absorption (beneficial if supplementing iron)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Beneficial**: Enhances iron absorption
- **Caution**: Kidney stones (history of calcium oxalate stones—limit to <1,000 mg)
- **Note**: High doses can cause diarrhea (reduce dose if occurs)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Immune**: 3-7 days (if illness), preventive benefits over months
- **Skin/collagen**: 6-12 weeks
- **Wound healing**: 2-4 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**MODERATE-STRONG** - Strong for scurvy prevention, immune support; moderate for colds (reduces duration by ~1 day if taken at onset)

### Cost
$5-15/month

# MINERALS

## Magnesium

### What It Is
Essential mineral involved in 300+ enzymatic reactions: energy production, muscle/nerve function, blood pressure, blood sugar control, sleep, stress response.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Chronic stress, poor sleep, athletes, high calcium intake, PPI use, diabetes, alcohol consumption
- **Symptoms**: Muscle cramps, eye twitching, insomnia, anxiety, constipation, migraines
- **Testing**: RBC Magnesium <5.0 mg/dL (serum unreliable)

### Best Form (Form Matters Greatly)
| Form | Absorption | Best For | Avoid If | Cost |
|------|------------|----------|----------|------|
| **Magnesium Glycinate** | Excellent (40%) | Sleep, anxiety, cramps (no laxative effect) | None | $$ |
| **Magnesium Citrate** | Good (30%) | Constipation (mild laxative) | Diarrhea-prone | $ |
| **Magnesium Threonate** | Good (crosses BBB) | Cognitive function, memory | Budget-limited | $$$ |
| **Magnesium Malate** | Good | Energy, fibromyalgia | None | $$ |
| **Magnesium Taurate** | Good | Cardiovascular health | None | $$ |
| **Magnesium Oxide** | Poor (4%) | **AVOID** | Always | $ |

### Dosing
- **General**: 200-400 mg elemental magnesium daily
- **Deficiency**: 400-600 mg daily
- **Sleep**: 300-500 mg glycinate before bed
- **Athletic**: 400-800 mg daily (split doses)
- **Upper Limit**: 350 mg from supplements (more from food OK)

### Timing & Absorption
- **Best time**: Evening or before bed (promotes relaxation)
- **Can split**: AM + PM if taking >400 mg
- **Separate from**: Calcium (2 hours), Iron (2 hours)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Beneficial**: Vitamin D (D needs Mg for activation), Calcium (balance)
- **Competes with**: Calcium, Iron (separate by 2 hours)
- **Caution**: Kidney disease (accumulation risk)
- **Medications**: Can affect absorption of antibiotics, bisphosphonates (separate by 2-4 hours)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Sleep**: 3-7 days
- **Muscle cramps**: 1-2 weeks
- **Anxiety**: 2-4 weeks
- **Blood pressure**: 4-8 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Robust evidence for sleep, muscle function, blood pressure, migraines, anxiety

### Cost
$10-20/month

## Iron

### What It Is
Essential mineral for oxygen transport (hemoglobin), energy production (mitochondria), immune function, cognitive function.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Menstruating females, pregnancy, vegetarians, blood donors, athletes, GI bleeding
- **Symptoms**: Fatigue, shortness of breath, pale skin, brittle nails, hair loss, restless legs, ice cravings (pica)
- **Testing**: Ferritin <30 ng/mL (deficient), 30-75 ng/mL (suboptimal), >300 ng/mL (overload)

### Best Form
| Form | Elemental Iron % | Absorption | Tolerability | Cost |
|------|------------------|------------|--------------|------|
| **Ferrous Bisglycinate** | 25% | Excellent | **Excellent** (chelated, gentle) | $$ |
| **Ferrous Sulfate** | 20% | High | Poor (GI upset, constipation) | $ |
| **Ferrous Fumarate** | 33% | High | Poor (GI upset) | $ |
| **Carbonyl Iron** | 70% | Moderate | Good | $$ |
| **Heme Iron Polypeptide** | 15-20% | Excellent | Excellent | $$$ |

### Dosing
- **Mild deficiency**: 25-40 mg elemental iron daily
- **Moderate-severe deficiency**: 65 mg elemental iron daily
- **Pregnancy**: 27-30 mg elemental iron daily (in prenatal)
- **Upper Limit**: 45 mg elemental (100-150 mg as iron salt)

**Note**: Read labels carefully—"325 mg ferrous sulfate" contains only 65 mg elemental iron

### Timing & Absorption
- **Best**: Empty stomach for maximum absorption (but causes nausea)
- **Practical**: With small meal + 500 mg Vitamin C (Vitamin C enhances absorption)
- **Separate from**: Calcium (2-4h), Zinc (2h), Magnesium (2h), Coffee/tea (2h), Antacids (2h)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Enhancer**: Vitamin C (take 500 mg with iron)
- **Inhibitors**: Calcium, zinc, magnesium, coffee, tea, antacids
- **Critical**: NEVER supplement iron without testing ferritin (overload risk)
- **Caution**: Hemochromatosis (genetic iron overload—NEVER supplement)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Energy**: 4-8 weeks
- **Hemoglobin normalization**: 2-3 months
- **Ferritin normalization**: 3-6 months

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Essential treatment for iron deficiency anemia

### Cost
$10-20/month

### **CRITICAL WARNING**
Only supplement iron if deficiency confirmed by testing. Iron overload is dangerous. Retest ferritin every 3 months. Stop when ferritin >75 ng/mL.

## Zinc

### What It Is
Essential mineral for immune function, wound healing, protein synthesis, DNA synthesis, taste/smell, testosterone production.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Vegetarians/vegans, alcohol consumption, malabsorption disorders, pregnancy
- **Symptoms**: Frequent infections, slow wound healing, hair loss, loss of taste/smell, white spots on nails
- **Testing**: Plasma Zinc 70-120 mcg/dL

### Best Form
| Form | Absorption | Best For | Cost |
|------|------------|----------|------|
| **Zinc Bisglycinate (Chelated)** | Highest | General supplementation (gentle on stomach) | $$ |
| **Zinc Picolinate** | High | General supplementation | $$ |
| **Zinc Gluconate** | Moderate | Lozenges for colds | $ |
| **Zinc Sulfate** | Moderate | Budget option | $ |
| **Zinc Oxide** | Poor | **AVOID** for oral use (topical sunscreen only) | $ |

### Dosing
- **General**: 15-30 mg elemental zinc daily
- **Deficiency**: 30-50 mg daily (short-term)
- **Acute cold**: 50-75 mg daily (lozenges) for 5-7 days
- **Upper Limit**: 40 mg daily long-term (higher doses deplete copper)

**CRITICAL**: Always supplement copper (1-2 mg) if taking zinc >30 mg long-term

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: With food (reduces nausea) or afternoon snack
- **Separate from**: Calcium (2h), Iron (2h), Antibiotics (2h)

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Depletes**: Copper (supplement 1-2 mg copper per 15-30 mg zinc long-term)
- **Competes with**: Calcium, Iron, Phytates (grains/legumes)
- **Caution**: Long-term high-dose (>50 mg) can cause copper deficiency anemia

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Immune (cold duration)**: 3-7 days
- **Wound healing**: 2-4 weeks
- **Taste/smell**: 4-8 weeks
- **Skin/hair**: 6-12 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Well-established for immune function, wound healing, deficiency correction

### Cost
$10-15/month

# OMEGA-3 FATTY ACIDS

## Omega-3 (EPA & DHA)

### What It Is
Essential polyunsaturated fatty acids (EPA=eicosapentaenoic acid, DHA=docosahexaenoic acid). Anti-inflammatory, support brain structure, cardiovascular health, mood regulation.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Low fish intake (<2 servings fatty fish/week), vegetarian/vegan, inflammation, mood disorders
- **Symptoms**: Dry skin, poor focus, mood issues, joint pain, inflammation
- **Testing**: Omega-3 Index <8% (deficient), 8-12% (optimal)

### Best Form
| Form | Absorption | Stability | Cost |
|------|------------|-----------|------|
| **Triglyceride (TG)** | High (60% better than EE) | Excellent | $$ |
| **Re-esterified Triglyceride (rTG)** | Highest | Excellent | $$$ |
| **Ethyl Ester (EE)** | Low | Moderate | $ |
| **Phospholipid (Krill Oil)** | High (different pathway) | Good | $$$ |

**Key**: Look for "triglyceride form" on label. Check **IFOS 5-star rating** for purity.

### Dosing
| Omega-3 Index | Goal | Dose (EPA+DHA combined) |
|---------------|------|-------------------------|
| <4% | Therapeutic | 2,000-3,000 mg |
| 4-8% | Improvement | 1,000-2,000 mg |
| 8-12% | Maintenance | 500-1,000 mg |

**Specific Conditions**:
- **Depression/Anxiety**: 1,000-2,000 mg EPA daily (EPA > DHA)
- **Inflammation**: 2,000-3,000 mg EPA+DHA
- **Cardiovascular**: 1,000-2,000 mg EPA+DHA
- **Pregnancy**: 300-600 mg DHA

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: With largest meal (breakfast or dinner)
- **With**: Meal containing fat
- **Note**: Taking with food reduces fishy burps

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Caution**: High-dose (>3g) + blood thinners → bleeding risk (monitor)
- **Stop**: 7-14 days before surgery (anticoagulant effect)
- **Safe**: <2g daily with blood thinners (discuss with doctor)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Mood**: 8-12 weeks
- **Inflammation**: 8-12 weeks
- **Skin/hair**: 8-12 weeks
- **Cardiovascular**: 12+ months

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Extensive research on cardiovascular health, mood, inflammation, cognitive function

### Cost
$20-40/month (quality matters—cheap fish oil often rancid)

# GUT HEALTH

## Probiotics

### What It Is
Live beneficial bacteria that support digestive health, immune function, mood (gut-brain axis), and microbiome diversity.

### Who Needs It
- **High Risk**: Recent antibiotics, digestive issues (IBS, bloating, diarrhea), frequent infections, mood issues
- **Symptoms**: Bloating, gas, irregular bowel movements, food sensitivities, frequent infections

### Best Form
- **Multi-strain**: 10-50 billion CFU (colony-forming units)
- **Key strains**: Lactobacillus and Bifidobacterium species
- **Delivery**: Enteric-coated or acid-resistant capsules (survive stomach acid)

**Strain Selection by Goal**:
| Goal | Best Strains |
|------|--------------|
| **General gut health** | L. acidophilus, L. rhamnosus, B. longum, B. bifidum |
| **IBS/Bloating** | B. infantis, L. plantarum, B. coagulans |
| **Diarrhea** | S. boulardii, L. rhamnosus GG |
| **Constipation** | B. lactis, L. casei |
| **Immune** | L. rhamnosus, L. casei, B. longum |
| **Mood** | L. helveticus, B. longum (psychobiotics) |

### Dosing
- **General**: 10-50 billion CFU daily
- **Post-antibiotic**: 50-100 billion CFU daily
- **Therapeutic (IBS, etc.)**: 25-100 billion CFU daily

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Strain-dependent—check product label
- **Common**: Morning with breakfast OR on empty stomach
- **After antibiotics**: Take 2 hours after antibiotic dose

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Caution**: Immunocompromised (risk of infection—discuss with doctor)
- **Safe**: Generally safe for most people

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Digestive**: 2-4 weeks
- **Immune**: 4-12 weeks
- **Mood**: 8-12 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**MODERATE-STRONG** - Strong for specific conditions (antibiotic-associated diarrhea, IBS), moderate for general health

### Cost
$20-40/month

# ADAPTOGEN & STRESS SUPPORT

## Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera)

### What It Is
Adaptogenic herb that modulates stress response by reducing cortisol, supporting HPA axis, enhancing GABA signaling.

### Who Needs It
- **Indications**: Chronic stress, anxiety, high cortisol, poor stress resilience, sleep issues
- **Symptoms**: Feeling "wired and tired," anxious, poor stress tolerance, burnout

### Best Form
- **KSM-66**: Most researched extract (5% withanolides)
- **Sensoril**: Higher withanolide content (10%), may be more sedating
- **Root extract** > Leaf extract

### Dosing
- **General**: 300-600 mg daily
- **Stress/Anxiety**: 300 mg AM + 300 mg PM (or 600 mg once daily)
- **Sleep**: 600 mg before bed (if sedating for you)
- **Duration**: 8-12 weeks minimum; can cycle (8 weeks on, 2 weeks off)

### Timing & Absorption
- **Typically**: Morning (or split AM/PM)
- **Some find sedating**: Before bed if you experience sedation
- **With food**: Reduces potential GI upset

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Caution**: Thyroid disorders (can increase thyroid hormones—monitor)
- **Avoid**: Pregnancy, autoimmune conditions (immune-modulating)
- **Medications**: May potentiate sedatives, thyroid medications

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Cortisol reduction**: 4-8 weeks (15-30% reduction in studies)
- **Stress/anxiety reduction**: 2-4 weeks
- **Sleep quality**: 2-4 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**MODERATE-STRONG** - Good evidence for stress reduction, cortisol lowering, anxiety

### Cost
$15-25/month

## Rhodiola rosea

### What It Is
Adaptogenic herb that enhances stress resilience, reduces fatigue, improves mental performance under stress.

### Who Needs It
- **Indications**: Fatigue, stress, burnout, need for mental/physical performance under pressure
- **Symptoms**: Chronic fatigue, poor stress tolerance, mental fog during stress

### Best Form
- **Standardized extract**: 3% rosavins + 1% salidroside (typical ratio in root)
- **Dose form**: Capsules or tablets

### Dosing
- **General**: 200-400 mg daily
- **Fatigue**: 400-600 mg daily (AM or split AM/Midday)
- **Take early**: Energizing; can interfere with sleep if taken late

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Morning or early afternoon (before 2 PM)
- **Avoid evening**: Can be stimulating

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Generally safe**: Few interactions
- **Caution**: Bipolar disorder (may trigger mania), pregnancy

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Fatigue reduction**: 2-4 weeks
- **Stress resilience**: 2-4 weeks
- **Mental performance**: 2-4 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**MODERATE** - Good evidence for fatigue and stress, but fewer large studies than Ashwagandha

### Cost
$15-25/month

# METABOLIC & BLOOD SUGAR SUPPORT

## Berberine

### What It Is
Plant alkaloid that activates AMPK enzyme, improves insulin sensitivity, lowers blood sugar, supports metabolic health. Often called "natural metformin."

### Who Needs It
- **Indications**: Prediabetes, type 2 diabetes, insulin resistance, metabolic syndrome, PCOS
- **Symptoms**: High fasting glucose, HbA1c >5.7%, difficulty losing weight, PCOS symptoms

### Best Form
- **Berberine HCl**: Standard form, well-absorbed
- **Dihydroberberine**: Newer form with better absorption (can use lower dose)

### Dosing
- **Standard**: 500 mg 2-3x daily with meals (total 1,000-1,500 mg)
- **Critical**: Must take with meals (reduces blood sugar—hypoglycemia risk on empty stomach)

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: With breakfast, lunch, dinner (if 3x) or breakfast + dinner (if 2x)
- **Why**: Lowers post-meal blood sugar; prevents hypoglycemia

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **CAUTION**: Diabetes medications (additive blood sugar lowering—monitor closely; may need medication dose reduction)
- **Avoid**: Pregnancy, breastfeeding
- **Monitor**: Blood glucose frequently when starting

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Blood sugar**: 2-4 weeks (lowered fasting glucose, HbA1c)
- **Weight loss**: 8-12 weeks (modest effect)
- **PCOS**: 8-12 weeks (improved ovulation, insulin sensitivity)

### Evidence Strength
**STRONG** - Robust evidence for blood sugar control, insulin sensitivity

### Cost
$15-25/month

### Best Form

### Dosing
- **General**: 200-400 mcg daily

### Timing & Absorption

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Caution**: Diabetes medications (monitor blood sugar)

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Blood sugar control**: 4-8 weeks
- **Carb cravings**: 2-4 weeks

### Evidence Strength
**MODERATE** - Some evidence for blood sugar control, especially in diabetes

# COGNITIVE & NOOTROPIC

## Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus)

### What It Is
Medicinal mushroom that stimulates nerve growth factor (NGF), supports neuroplasticity, cognitive function, and neuroprotection.

### Who Needs It
- **Indications**: Cognitive decline, memory issues, neuroprotection, focus
- **Interest**: Brain health optimization, neuroplasticity

### Best Form
- **Dual extract**: Hot water + alcohol extraction (captures polysaccharides + triterpenes)
- **Fruiting body** > Mycelium (more active compounds)

### Dosing
- **General**: 500-3,000 mg daily (varies widely by extract concentration)
- **Common**: 1,000-1,500 mg daily

### Timing & Absorption
- **When**: Morning or anytime
- **With/without food**: Either is fine

### Interactions & Contraindications
- **Generally safe**: Few interactions
- **Caution**: Mushroom allergies

### Expected Benefits & Timeline
- **Cognitive function**: 8-12 weeks (long-term neuroplasticity effects)
- **Neuroprotection**: Ongoing (preventive)

### Evidence Strength
**WEAK-MODERATE** - Promising animal studies; limited human studies

### Cost
$20-40/month

## Best Forms Guide

| Supplement | BEST Form | AVOID Form | Why |
|------------|-----------|------------|-----|
| **Magnesium** | Glycinate, Citrate, Threonate | Oxide | Oxide = 4% absorption |
