# Supplement Special Conditions Framework

Use this framework for evidence-based supplement strategies tailored to specific health conditions and therapeutic goals.

## Core Concept

The Supplement Special Conditions Framework provides condition-specific supplement protocols based on research evidence, clinical practice, and mechanism of action. Each protocol includes recommended supplements, dosing, evidence grades, medical supervision requirements, and expected outcomes.

## When to Apply

- When managing a specific diagnosed health condition
- To complement medical treatment (not replace it)
- When researching supplement options for a condition
- Before discussing supplement protocol with healthcare provider
- When evaluating condition-specific supplement recommendations
- To understand evidence strength for specific uses

## Critical Principles

### Principle 1: Supplements Complement, Don't Replace Medical Care
- Use supplements as adjunct to medical treatment
- Never stop prescribed medications without medical supervision
- Supplements can reduce medication needs over time (adjust with doctor)
- Some conditions require medication; supplements alone are insufficient

### Principle 2: Evidence Grades Matter
- **Strong Evidence**: Multiple RCTs, meta-analyses showing benefit
- **Moderate Evidence**: Some RCTs, mechanistic support
- **Weak/Preliminary Evidence**: Small studies, animal studies, mechanistic rationale
- Prioritize supplements with strong evidence for your condition

### Principle 3: Medical Supervision for Complex Conditions
- Autoimmune, cardiovascular, diabetes, thyroid, mental health conditions require medical monitoring
- Supplements can interact with medications
- Some supplements affect lab values
- Regular monitoring ensures safety and efficacy

### Principle 4: Personalization Required
- Protocols are starting points, not prescriptions
- Individual needs vary based on severity, medications, other conditions
- Start with highest-evidence supplements, add others as needed
- Track response and adjust

# METABOLIC CONDITIONS

## Metabolic Syndrome / Insulin Resistance / Prediabetes

### Condition Overview
Cluster of conditions: insulin resistance, high blood sugar, abdominal obesity, high triglycerides, low HDL, high blood pressure.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Berberine** | 500 mg 2-3x daily with meals | AMPK activation, improves insulin sensitivity | **STRONG** | $15-25 |
| **Magnesium** | 300-400 mg daily | Insulin sensitivity, glucose metabolism | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Omega-3** | 2,000-3,000 mg EPA+DHA | Reduces inflammation, improves insulin sensitivity | **STRONG** | $30-40 |
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (test first) | Insulin sensitivity, inflammation | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $10-15 |

#### Tier 2: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Chromium Picolinate** | 400-1,000 mcg daily | Enhances insulin signaling | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |
| **Alpha-Lipoic Acid** | 300-600 mg daily | Insulin sensitivity, glucose uptake | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $20-30 |
| **Inositol** (for PCOS) | 2-4g daily | Insulin sensitivity | **STRONG** (for PCOS) | $20-30 |
| **Cinnamon Extract** | 500 mg or 1-6g powder daily | Glucose metabolism | **WEAK-MODERATE** | $10-15 |

### Protocol Strategy
**Start with**: Berberine + Magnesium + Omega-3
**Add if needed**: Vitamin D (if deficient), Alpha-lipoic acid
**Monitor**: Fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin every 3 months

### Medical Supervision
**Required**: Yes—monitor blood sugar closely, especially if on diabetes medications (hypoglycemia risk)

### Expected Timeline
- **Blood sugar improvement**: 4-12 weeks
- **HbA1c reduction**: 3-6 months
- **Weight loss (if applicable)**: 8-12 weeks (modest effect)

### Safety Notes
- Berberine can lower blood sugar significantly—monitor closely
- May allow medication dose reduction over time (work with doctor)
- Hypoglycemia symptoms: shakiness, sweating, confusion → check blood sugar

## Type 2 Diabetes

### Condition Overview
Chronic condition of insulin resistance and elevated blood glucose requiring medical management.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence (Use with Medications)
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Notes |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Berberine** | 500 mg 2-3x daily with meals | Lowers blood sugar (metformin-like) | **STRONG** | Monitor glucose closely |
| **Alpha-Lipoic Acid** | 600-1,200 mg daily | Improves insulin sensitivity, neuropathy | **STRONG** | Higher doses for neuropathy |
| **Chromium Picolinate** | 600-1,000 mcg daily | Enhances insulin action | **MODERATE-STRONG** | Check levels first |
| **Magnesium** | 400-600 mg daily | Often deficient in diabetes | **STRONG** | Check RBC magnesium |
| **Omega-3** | 2,000-3,000 mg EPA+DHA | Cardiovascular protection, inflammation | **STRONG** | Especially if high triglycerides |

### Protocol Strategy
**Essential**: Continue all prescribed medications
**Add**: Berberine + Alpha-lipoic acid + Magnesium + Omega-3
**Monitor**: Blood glucose 3-4x daily initially; HbA1c every 3 months

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—close glucose monitoring, medication adjustment needed

### Expected Timeline
- **Blood sugar**: 2-4 weeks
- **HbA1c**: 3-6 months (expect 0.5-1.5% reduction)
- **Neuropathy** (with ALA): 6-12 months

### Safety Notes
- Supplements can significantly lower blood sugar → hypoglycemia risk with medications
- May allow gradual medication reduction (work with endocrinologist)
- Alpha-lipoic acid can cause low blood sugar—start low, monitor

# CARDIOVASCULAR CONDITIONS

## High Blood Pressure (Hypertension)

### Condition Overview
Chronic elevation of blood pressure requiring management to prevent cardiovascular events.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Notes |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Magnesium** | 400-600 mg daily | Vasodilation, relaxes blood vessels | **STRONG** | Check RBC Magnesium |
| **Omega-3** | 2,000-3,000 mg EPA+DHA | Reduces inflammation, improves endothelial function | **STRONG** | Especially high triglycerides |
| **CoQ10** (ubiquinol if >40) | 100-200 mg daily | Improves endothelial function | **MODERATE-STRONG** | Especially if on statins |
| **Potassium** (food preferred) | 2,000-4,000 mg from food | Balances sodium, lowers BP | **STRONG** | Only supplement if tested low |

#### Tier 2: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Notes |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Garlic (Aged Garlic Extract)** | 600-1,200 mg daily | Vasodilation, nitric oxide | **MODERATE** | Modest effect (5-10 mmHg) |
| **L-Arginine** | 3-6g daily | Nitric oxide precursor | **MODERATE** | Variable response |
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (if low) | BP regulation | **WEAK-MODERATE** | Test first |

### Protocol Strategy
**Start with**: Magnesium + Omega-3
**Add if on statins**: CoQ10
**Add if BP still high**: Garlic extract

**DO NOT STOP** blood pressure medications without medical supervision

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—monitor BP regularly, medication adjustment needed

### Expected Timeline
- **BP reduction**: 4-12 weeks (modest: 5-10 mmHg systolic)
- Supplements complement medications; usually don't replace them

### Safety Notes
- Supplements reduce BP modestly; medications usually still needed
- Monitor BP at home regularly (2x daily)
- Report dizziness, lightheadedness (BP too low)
- Potassium supplements contraindicated with ACE inhibitors/ARBs (hyperkalemia risk)

## High Cholesterol / Cardiovascular Risk

### Condition Overview
Elevated LDL cholesterol, low HDL, or high triglycerides increasing cardiovascular risk.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Omega-3** (high EPA) | 2,000-4,000 mg EPA+DHA | Lowers triglycerides 20-30% | **STRONG** | $40-60 |
| **Niacin (Vitamin B3)** | 500-2,000 mg daily (extended-release) | Raises HDL, lowers LDL | **STRONG** | $15-30 |
| **Plant Sterols/Stanols** | 2g daily | Blocks cholesterol absorption | **STRONG** | $20-30 |
| **Psyllium Fiber** | 10-20g daily | Binds bile acids, lowers LDL | **STRONG** | $10-20 |
| **CoQ10** (if on statins) | 100-200 mg daily | Replenishes CoQ10 depleted by statins | **STRONG** | $20-30 |

#### Tier 2: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Berberine** | 500 mg 2-3x daily | Lowers LDL, blood sugar | **MODERATE** | $15-25 |
| **Garlic (Aged Garlic Extract)** | 600-1,200 mg daily | Modest LDL lowering | **MODERATE** | $15-25 |
| **Red Yeast Rice** | 1,200-2,400 mg daily | Contains natural statin (monacolin K) | **STRONG*** | $20-30 |

*Red Yeast Rice is essentially a natural statin—**DO NOT combine with statin medications**

### Protocol Strategy
**If high triglycerides**: Omega-3 (high dose)
**If high LDL**: Plant sterols + Psyllium fiber
**If on statins**: Add CoQ10 (100-200 mg)
**If not on statins and high LDL**: Consider Red Yeast Rice (but discuss with doctor—it IS a statin)

### Medical Supervision
**Recommended**: Yes—especially if considering Red Yeast Rice or high-dose niacin

### Expected Timeline
- **Triglycerides**: 6-12 weeks (20-30% reduction with Omega-3)
- **LDL**: 4-8 weeks (5-15% reduction with plant sterols/fiber)
- **HDL**: 8-12 weeks (5-15% increase with niacin)

### Safety Notes
- **Niacin**: Causes "flushing" (red, hot skin)—use extended-release or take aspirin 30 min before
- **Red Yeast Rice**: Is a statin—do NOT combine with prescription statins; monitor liver enzymes
- **CoQ10**: Essential if on statins (statins deplete CoQ10, causing muscle pain)

# THYROID CONDITIONS

## Hypothyroidism (Underactive Thyroid)

### Condition Overview
Insufficient thyroid hormone production; requires thyroid hormone replacement (levothyroxine).

### Supplement Protocol

#### Supportive Nutrients (NOT Replacements for Medication)
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Notes |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Selenium** | 200 mcg daily | Required for T4→T3 conversion | **STRONG** | Especially Hashimoto's |
| **Zinc** | 15-30 mg daily | Required for thyroid hormone synthesis | **MODERATE** | Test first |
| **Iron** (if deficient) | 25-65 mg elemental | Required for thyroid hormone production | **STRONG** | Test ferritin first |
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (if low) | Immune modulation (Hashimoto's) | **MODERATE** | Test first |

#### AVOID (Can Worsen Hypothyroidism)
| Supplement | Why Avoid |
|------------|-----------|
| **Iodine (high-dose)** | Can worsen hypothyroidism or trigger hyperthyroidism; use only if iodine-deficient |
| **Soy Isoflavones** | May interfere with thyroid hormone absorption |
| **Calcium, Iron** (at same time as thyroid med) | Block absorption of levothyroxine—separate by 4+ hours |

### Protocol Strategy
**Essential**: Continue thyroid medication (levothyroxine)
**Add**: Selenium (especially if Hashimoto's) + Vitamin D (if low) + Iron/Zinc (if deficient)
**Timing**: Take thyroid medication first thing AM on empty stomach; wait 4 hours before calcium/iron supplements

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—thyroid levels (TSH, Free T3, Free T4) monitored every 6-12 weeks

### Expected Timeline
- **Thyroid antibodies** (Hashimoto's with selenium): 3-6 months (reduction in TPO antibodies)
- Supplements support thyroid function but don't replace hormone

### Safety Notes
- **DO NOT supplement iodine** without testing urinary iodine first (can worsen autoimmune thyroid disease)
- Separate supplements from thyroid medication by 4+ hours
- Selenium >400 mcg daily is toxic

## Hashimoto's Thyroiditis (Autoimmune Hypothyroidism)

### Condition Overview
Autoimmune attack on thyroid gland causing hypothyroidism; requires thyroid hormone replacement + immune modulation.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Selenium** | 200 mcg daily | Reduces TPO antibodies 20-40% | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (test first) | Immune modulation, reduces autoimmunity | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Omega-3** | 1,000-2,000 mg EPA+DHA | Anti-inflammatory, immune modulation | **MODERATE** | $30-40 |

#### Tier 2: Supportive Nutrients
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Zinc** | 15-30 mg (test first) | Thyroid hormone synthesis | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |
| **Iron** (if deficient) | 25-65 mg elemental | Thyroid hormone production | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Magnesium** | 300-400 mg | Supports thyroid function | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |

#### AVOID (Can Trigger Autoimmune Flares)
| Supplement | Why Avoid |
|------------|-----------|
| **Iodine (>150 mcg)** | Can trigger or worsen Hashimoto's flares |
| **Echinacea, Astragalus** | Immune-stimulating—may worsen autoimmune activity |

### Protocol Strategy
**Start with**: Selenium + Vitamin D (if low) + Omega-3
**Add if deficient**: Iron, Zinc
**Avoid**: High-dose iodine, immune-stimulating herbs

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—monitor TSH, Free T3, Free T4, TPO antibodies every 3-6 months

### Expected Timeline
- **TPO antibody reduction**: 3-6 months (20-40% reduction with selenium)
- **Symptom improvement**: 8-12 weeks (if thyroid hormone optimized)

### Safety Notes
- Selenium is critical but toxic above 400 mcg—don't exceed dose
- Iodine supplementation controversial; only if urinary iodine low
- Continue thyroid hormone replacement (supplements don't replace it)

# AUTOIMMUNE CONDITIONS

## General Autoimmune Support (RA, Lupus, MS, etc.)

### Condition Overview
Immune system attacking body's own tissues; requires medical management + lifestyle/supplement support.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence for Autoimmune Inflammation
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (target 50-80 ng/mL) | Immune modulation (not stimulation) | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Omega-3** | 2,000-3,000 mg EPA+DHA | Anti-inflammatory, reduces cytokines | **STRONG** | $30-40 |
| **Curcumin** | 500-1,500 mg daily (with piperine) | NF-κB inhibition, reduces inflammation | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $20-40 |

#### Tier 2: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Vitamin D** + **Vitamin K2** | As above + 200 mcg K2 | Immune modulation + bone protection | **MODERATE** | $15-20 |
| **Selenium** | 200 mcg daily | Antioxidant, immune regulation | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |
| **Probiotics** | 25-50 billion CFU | Gut-immune axis modulation | **MODERATE** | $20-30 |

#### AVOID (Can Worsen Autoimmune Activity)
| Supplement | Why Avoid |
|------------|-----------|
| **Echinacea** | Immune-stimulating—may trigger flares |
| **Astragalus** | Immune-stimulating—may trigger flares |
| **DHEA** (unless prescribed) | May worsen autoimmune activity |

### Protocol Strategy
**Start with**: Vitamin D (optimize to 50-80 ng/mL) + Omega-3
**Add**: Curcumin (especially if joint pain/inflammation)
**Avoid**: Immune-stimulating herbs

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—coordinate with rheumatologist, neurologist, or specialist

### Expected Timeline
- **Inflammation reduction**: 8-12 weeks (CRP, ESR may decrease)
- **Symptom improvement**: Variable (12-24 weeks)
- Supplements complement medications, don't replace them

### Safety Notes
- **DO NOT stop immunosuppressant medications**
- Avoid immune-stimulating supplements (can trigger flares)
- Some biologics may interact with supplements—discuss with rheumatologist

# MENTAL HEALTH CONDITIONS

## Depression

### Condition Overview
Mood disorder; supplements complement (not replace) medications and therapy.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Strong Evidence (Adjunct to Treatment)
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Omega-3 (EPA-rich)** | 1,000-2,000 mg EPA daily (EPA>DHA ratio) | Anti-inflammatory, brain cell membrane | **STRONG** | $30-40 |
| **Vitamin D** | 2,000-5,000 IU (if <30 ng/mL) | Mood regulation, neurotransmitter synthesis | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Methylfolate** (5-MTHF) | 7.5-15 mg daily | Supports neurotransmitter synthesis | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $30-50 |
| **SAMe** | 400-1,600 mg daily | Methyl donor, supports neurotransmitters | **MODERATE-STRONG** | $30-60 |

#### Tier 2: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Magnesium** | 300-400 mg daily | NMDA receptor modulation, calming | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |
| **Zinc** | 15-30 mg daily (test first) | Neurotransmitter function | **MODERATE** | $10-15 |
| **B-Complex** | B-50 or B-100 | Neurotransmitter synthesis | **MODERATE** | $10-20 |

#### AVOID if on Antidepressants (Serotonin Syndrome Risk)
| Supplement | Why Avoid |
|------------|-----------|
| **5-HTP** | Serotonin syndrome risk with SSRIs/SNRIs |
| **St. John's Wort** | Drug interactions (reduces antidepressant efficacy), serotonin syndrome risk |
| **L-Tryptophan** | Serotonin syndrome risk |

### Protocol Strategy
**Start with**: Omega-3 (EPA-rich) + Vitamin D (if low)
**Add if on antidepressant with partial response**: Methylfolate OR SAMe (discuss with psychiatrist)
**Safe with antidepressants**: Omega-3, Vitamin D, Magnesium, B-complex

### Medical Supervision
**REQUIRED**: Yes—coordinate with psychiatrist or primary care provider

### Expected Timeline
- **Omega-3**: 8-12 weeks
- **Vitamin D**: 8-12 weeks (if deficient)
- **SAMe/Methylfolate**: 4-8 weeks (as augmentation)

### Safety Notes
- **DO NOT stop antidepressants** to try supplements
- **NEVER combine** 5-HTP, St. John's Wort, or L-Tryptophan with SSRIs/SNRIs (serotonin syndrome)
- Methylfolate and SAMe can augment antidepressants—discuss with psychiatrist
- Supplements work best as adjunct, not monotherapy

# OSTEOPOROSIS / BONE HEALTH

### Condition Overview
Low bone density increasing fracture risk; requires long-term strategy.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Essential Triad
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Vitamin D3** | 1,000-2,000 IU (maintain 50-80 ng/mL) | Enhances calcium absorption | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Vitamin K2 (MK-7)** | 100-200 mcg daily | Directs calcium to bones (not arteries) | **STRONG** | $15-20 |
| **Calcium** (if diet insufficient) | 500-1,000 mg daily (split doses) | Bone structure | **STRONG** | $10-15 |

#### Supportive Nutrients
| Supplement | Dose | Mechanism | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Magnesium** | 300-400 mg daily | Bone mineralization, activates Vitamin D | **STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Boron** | 3 mg daily | Enhances calcium/magnesium metabolism | **MODERATE** | $5-10 |
| **Vitamin C** | 500-1,000 mg daily | Collagen synthesis | **MODERATE** | $5-10 |
| **Collagen Peptides** | 10-15g daily | Bone matrix support | **MODERATE** | $30-40 |

### Protocol Strategy
**Essential**: Vitamin D3 + K2 + Calcium (if dietary intake <1,000 mg)
**Add**: Magnesium + Boron
**Consider**: Collagen peptides for additional support

### Dosing Notes
- **Calcium**: Max 500 mg per dose (better absorption); take morning + evening if >500 mg total
- **Don't overdo calcium**: Target 1,000-1,200 mg TOTAL (diet + supplements combined)

### Medical Supervision
**Required**: Yes—DEXA scans to monitor bone density (baseline, then every 1-2 years)

### Expected Timeline
- **Bone density improvement**: 12-24 months (slow process)
- **Fracture risk reduction**: Long-term (years)

### Safety Notes
- Vitamin K2 essential—without it, calcium can deposit in arteries (not bones)
- Don't mega-dose calcium (>1,200 mg total daily)—associated with cardiovascular risk
- Weight-bearing exercise critical (supplements alone insufficient)

# ATHLETIC PERFORMANCE

### Condition Overview
Enhancing strength, endurance, recovery, and body composition in athletes.

### Supplement Protocol

#### Tier 1: Very Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Benefit | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|---------|----------|------------|
| **Creatine Monohydrate** | 5g daily (maintenance) | Strength, power, muscle mass | **VERY STRONG** | $10-15 |
| **Caffeine** | 3-6 mg/kg body weight (200-400mg) | Performance, endurance, focus | **VERY STRONG** | $5-10 |
| **Protein Powder** | 20-40g post-workout | Muscle recovery, growth | **VERY STRONG** | $30-50 |

#### Tier 2: Strong Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Benefit | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|---------|----------|------------|
| **Beta-Alanine** | 2-5g daily | Endurance (buffers lactic acid) | **STRONG** | $15-20 |
| **Citrulline Malate** | 6-8g pre-workout | Pump, endurance (nitric oxide) | **STRONG** | $15-25 |

#### Tier 3: Moderate Evidence
| Supplement | Dose | Benefit | Evidence | Cost/Month |
|------------|------|---------|----------|------------|
| **BCAAs** (if fasted training) | 5-10g around workout | Muscle preservation | **MODERATE** | $20-30 |
| **HMB** (cutting/older adults) | 3g daily | Muscle preservation | **MODERATE** | $30-40 |
| **Beetroot Juice/Powder** | 500mg nitrates | Endurance (nitric oxide) | **MODERATE** | $20-30 |

### Protocol Strategy
**Essential for strength/power**: Creatine + Protein
**Add for endurance**: Beta-alanine + Citrulline
**Fasted training**: BCAAs
**Cutting (fat loss)**: HMB + Creatine

### Medical Supervision
**Not required** for healthy athletes

### Expected Timeline
- **Creatine**: 2-4 weeks (strength, size)
- **Beta-alanine**: 2-4 weeks (endurance)
- **Citrulline**: Acute (1-2 hours pre-workout)
- **Protein**: Ongoing recovery support

### Safety Notes
- Creatine is extensively studied and very safe
- Caffeine: Individual tolerance varies; avoid if sensitive
- Beta-alanine causes harmless tingling (paresthesia)
- Protein: ~0.7-1g per lb body weight total (diet + supplements)

## Limitations

- Protocols are starting points; individual needs vary
- Evidence strength is subjective and evolving
- Supplements complement medical treatment, don't replace it
- Dosing ranges are general; some need more or less
- Medical supervision requirements are guidelines; always consult your healthcare provider
- Some conditions require medications; supplements alone are insufficient
- Timeline estimates are averages; individual response varies
- Cost estimates vary by brand and region

**Remember**: These protocols support conventional medical treatment. Never stop prescribed medications without medical supervision. Work with your healthcare provider to integrate supplements safely and effectively. This framework is educational, not medical advice.
