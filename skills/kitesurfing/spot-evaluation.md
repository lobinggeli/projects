# Kitesurfing Spot Evaluation Framework

Use this framework to systematically assess new kitesurfing locations for safety, suitability, and optimal conditions.

## Core Concept

The Spot Evaluation Framework provides a structured methodology for analyzing kitesurfing locations across physical, environmental, and logistical dimensions. Proper spot assessment prevents accidents, optimizes session quality, and builds mental maps of safe riding zones.

### The Five Evaluation Criteria

1. **Launch & Landing Zones**: Space, surface, obstacles, crowd management
2. **Riding Area**: Water depth, size, hazards, boundaries
3. **Wind Characteristics**: Direction reliability, strength patterns, local effects
4. **Safety Infrastructure**: Rescue access, emergency services, communications
5. **Logistics**: Access, facilities, seasonality, regulations

## When to Apply

- First visit to any new location
- Scouting destinations before booking travel
- Seasonal reassessment of familiar spots (conditions change)
- After significant weather events (hurricanes, storms alter beaches)
- When bringing groups or teaching students
- Evaluating spots for different skill levels

## Spot Classification Matrix

| Spot Type | Characteristics | Skill Level | Example Conditions |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Beginner Paradise** | Shallow, flat water, steady wind, sandy bottom | Novice-Intermediate | Lagoon, bay, protected beach |
| **Freeride Heaven** | Open water, consistent wind, medium chop | Intermediate-Advanced | Coastal beaches, large bays |
| **Wave Spot** | Breaking waves, strong wind, varying depth | Advanced+ | Ocean beaches, reef breaks |
| **Freestyle Arena** | Flat water, strong steady wind, open space | Intermediate-Expert | Lagoons, lakes, specific beaches |
| **Extreme/Remote** | Challenging access, variable conditions, limited rescue | Expert only | Isolated beaches, expedition spots |

## Launch & Landing Assessment

### Launch Zone Checklist

**Space Requirements**:
- Minimum width: 30m clear space (wider for higher winds)
- Minimum depth: 50m from setup to water
- Clearance overhead: No power lines, trees within 100m upwind

**Surface Evaluation**:
- [ ] **Sand**: Soft (ideal) / Hard-packed (acceptable) / Rocky (problematic)
- [ ] **Slope**: Gradual (ideal) / Steep (difficult launch) / Cliff (dangerous)
- [ ] **Debris**: Clear / Minor sticks/shells / Major hazards (glass, metal)

**Obstacle Mapping**:
```
         LAND
    ╔════════════╗
    ║  Buildings ║
    ╚════════════╝
         Trees
    ★ Launch Zone ★  <-- Mark on mental map
    ----------------
       ← Wind →
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
        WATER
```

### Landing Zone Planning

**Primary Landing**:
- Same as launch (ideal) or clearly defined alternate
- Clear approach path (no obstacles downwind)
- Space to drop kite safely (100m+ from hazards)

**Emergency Landing**:
- Identify 2-3 backup beaches within reach
- Note distances and compass bearings
- Consider water current drift direction

## Riding Area Analysis

### Water Depth Mapping

| Depth Range | Suitability | Skill Requirement | Risk Factors |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **<0.5m (knee-deep)** | Learning area only | Beginner | Injury on fall, fin drag |
| **0.5-1m (waist-deep)** | Basic riding practice | Beginner-Intermediate | Standing possible, lower consequence |
| **1-3m (overhead)** | Comfortable riding | All levels | Ideal depth, safe falling |
| **>3m (deep)** | Open water riding | Intermediate+ | Self-rescue skills required |
| **>10m (very deep)** | Offshore/crossing | Advanced | Equipment failure = serious |

### Hazard Identification

**Underwater Hazards**:
- [ ] **Rocks/Reefs**: Map locations, depth, visibility
- [ ] **Sandbars**: Note breaks, channels, tide effects
- [ ] **Vegetation**: Seaweed, kelp beds (entanglement risk)
- [ ] **Man-made**: Moorings, pilings, submerged structures

**Above-water Hazards**:
- [ ] **Fixed**: Piers, jetties, breakwaters (map distances)
- [ ] **Mobile**: Boats, swimmers, other kiters (traffic patterns)
- [ ] **Tidal**: Exposed rocks, changing depths (note tide stages)

### Riding Zone Dimensions

- **Uncrowded**: <5 riders per km of beach (comfortable)
- **Moderate**: 5-15 riders per km (requires awareness)
- **Crowded**: >15 riders per km (high collision risk, avoid peak times)

## Limitations

- Conditions change seasonally and annually (erosion, construction)
- Tide and swell can dramatically alter a spot's character
- Local knowledge accumulates over multiple visits
- Social dynamics (locals, crowds) vary and aren't captured fully
- Regulatory changes (new restrictions) require ongoing monitoring

**Best Practice**: Always ask locals on first visit. Online research gets you there; local knowledge keeps you safe and finds the best conditions.
