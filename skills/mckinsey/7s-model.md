# McKinsey 7S Framework

Use this framework to analyze organizational effectiveness and alignment. The 7 elements are interconnected—changes in one affect the others.

## Hard Elements (Easier to define and manage)
- **Strategy**: The plan to achieve competitive advantage and meet objectives
- **Structure**: How the organization is organized (hierarchy, reporting lines, divisions)
- **Systems**: Daily processes, procedures, and workflows that drive operations

## Soft Elements (Less tangible, culture-driven)
- **Shared Values**: Core beliefs and values at the center of the organization (the hub connecting all elements)
- **Style**: Leadership approach and organizational culture
- **Staff**: Employees, their capabilities, and how they are developed
- **Skills**: Competencies and capabilities the organization possesses

## When to Apply
- Organizational change initiatives
- Mergers and acquisitions analysis
- Performance improvement projects
- Strategy implementation assessment
- Identifying misalignment between elements
