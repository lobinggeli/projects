# McKinsey Three Horizons of Growth

Use this framework to balance current performance while maximizing future growth opportunities. It helps organizations manage innovation across different time frames.

## The Three Horizons

### Horizon 1: Maintain & Defend Core Business
- **Focus**: Current cash-generating activities
- **Timeframe**: 0-12 months
- **Goal**: Optimize and extend existing business
- **Activities**: Improve efficiency, defend market share, incremental innovations
- **Risk**: Low
- **Returns**: Predictable, immediate

### Horizon 2: Nurture Emerging Opportunities
- **Focus**: Emerging businesses requiring investment
- **Timeframe**: 12-36 months
- **Goal**: Build new revenue streams
- **Activities**: Scale promising ventures, enter adjacent markets, develop new capabilities
- **Risk**: Medium
- **Returns**: Growing, near-term

### Horizon 3: Create Viable Future Options
- **Focus**: Ideas that could become future businesses
- **Timeframe**: 36-72 months
- **Goal**: Plant seeds for long-term growth
- **Activities**: Research, pilots, experiments, venture investments
- **Risk**: High
- **Returns**: Uncertain, long-term

## When to Apply
- Innovation portfolio planning
- R&D investment allocation
- Strategic planning sessions
- Balancing short-term vs long-term priorities
- Startup/venture evaluation within corporations

## Key Principles
1. **Simultaneous management**: All three horizons need attention concurrently
2. **Resource allocation**: Don't starve H2/H3 to feed H1
3. **Different metrics**: Each horizon requires different KPIs
4. **Migration path**: H3 → H2 → H1 over time
