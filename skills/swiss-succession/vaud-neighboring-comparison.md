# Vaud vs. Neighboring Cantons Comparison

Use this framework to compare succession tax rates, rules, and strategic opportunities between Canton de Vaud and its neighboring cantons (Geneva, Fribourg, Neuchâtel, Bern, Valais) for residence optimization and cross-border planning.

## Core Concept

The Vaud Neighboring Cantons Comparison Framework enables strategic succession planning by comparing tax systems across French-speaking Switzerland. With cantonal rates varying from 0% to 50%, understanding regional differences empowers residence decisions, asset structuring, and gifting strategies to minimize total tax burden while maintaining lifestyle compatibility.

### The Lake Geneva Tax Landscape

```
Cantonal Inheritance Tax Rates (Siblings inheriting CHF 1M)

[Schwyz]──[Fribourg]──[Vaud]──[Geneva]──[Valais]──[Neuchâtel]──[Bern]
   0%       ~CHF 90K   ~CHF 140K  ~CHF 150K  ~CHF 100K  ~CHF 150K   ~CHF 120K

Vaud position: High-tax canton, among most expensive for non-direct heirs
```

## When to Apply

- You are a Vaud resident considering relocation for tax optimization
- You own property in multiple cantons (Vaud + neighbors)
- You are choosing residence location with succession planning in mind
- You are comparing tax impact of living in Vaud vs. nearby cantons
- You need to understand cross-border tax treatment (e.g., Vaud resident owning Geneva property)
- You are advising international clients choosing Swiss residence canton

## Vaud vs. Geneva

### Tax Rate Comparison

**Similarities**:
- Both are **high-tax cantons** for non-direct heirs
- Both exempt spouses, registered partners, and direct descendants (0%)
- Both have progressive rates increasing with inheritance amount

**Rate comparison for common relationships**:

| Relationship | Vaud Tax (CHF 1M) | Geneva Tax (CHF 1M) | Difference |
|-------------|------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Spouse** | CHF 0 (0%) | CHF 0 (0%) | Equal |
| **Child** | CHF 0 (0%) | CHF 0 (0%) | Equal |
| **Sibling** | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | ~CHF 150,000 (15%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 10K |
| **Nephew/Niece** | ~CHF 220,000 (22%) | ~CHF 250,000 (25%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 30K |
| **Unmarried partner** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 400,000 (40%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 30K |
| **Non-relative** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 450,000 (45%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 80K |

**Key insight**: Geneva is **slightly more expensive** than Vaud for non-direct heirs, especially non-relatives (up to 45-50% in Geneva vs. 50% max in Vaud).

### Strategic Implications

**When Vaud is better than Geneva**:
- Non-relative or unmarried partner beneficiaries (unless registered partnership)
- Larger estates to distant relatives

**When Geneva and Vaud are equivalent**:
- Direct descendants or spouse beneficiaries (both 0%)
- Small estates where absolute difference is negligible

**When to choose based on non-tax factors**:
- **Lifestyle**: Geneva is international, cosmopolitan; Vaud more diverse (Lausanne urban, Lavaux/Riviera smaller)
- **Language**: Both French-speaking
- **Cost of living**: Geneva higher overall (housing, services)
- **Real estate**: Geneva property very expensive (lakefront, city center)

### Cross-Border Property Planning

**Scenario**: Vaud resident owns Geneva property (or vice versa)

**Tax treatment**:
- Geneva property → **Taxed by Geneva** (situs rule) regardless of owner residence
- Vaud property → **Taxed by Vaud** regardless of owner residence

**Example**:
- Vaud resident owns CHF 1,200,000 Geneva apartment
- Heir: Niece
- **Geneva taxes** the apartment at Geneva rates: ~CHF 300,000 tax
- **Vaud does NOT tax** (property not in Vaud)

**Implication**: Living in Vaud does not reduce tax on Geneva property. Consider selling Geneva property and reinvesting in financial assets (taxed at residence canton rate).

## Vaud vs. Fribourg

### Tax Rate Comparison

**Fribourg characteristics**:
- **Moderate-high tax canton**
- Progressive rates, but lower than Vaud for most relationships
- Bilingual canton (French and German) bordering Vaud

| Relationship | Vaud Tax (CHF 1M) | Fribourg Tax (CHF 1M) | Difference |
|-------------|------------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Spouse** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Child** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Sibling** | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | ~CHF 90,000 (9%) | **Fribourg cheaper by CHF 50K** |
| **Nephew/Niece** | ~CHF 220,000 (22%) | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | **Fribourg cheaper by CHF 80K** |
| **Unmarried partner** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 250,000 (25%) | **Fribourg cheaper by CHF 120K** |

**Key insight**: Fribourg is **significantly cheaper** than Vaud for non-direct heirs (savings of CHF 50-120K on CHF 1M estate).

### Strategic Implications

**When to consider Fribourg over Vaud**:
- **Significant estates to non-direct heirs** (siblings, nieces/nephews, unmarried partners)
- **Lifestyle compatible**: Fribourg borders Vaud, similar French-speaking culture (plus bilingual German)
- **Real estate costs**: Fribourg generally less expensive than Lausanne/Vaud lakefront

**Relocation ROI example**:
- Vaud resident, CHF 2,000,000 estate to sibling
- **Vaud tax**: ~CHF 380,000
- **Fribourg tax** (if relocate): ~CHF 180,000
- **Savings**: CHF 200,000
- **Relocation cost**: Modest (moving expenses, lifestyle adjustment within same region)

**Limitations**:
- Must establish **genuine residence** in Fribourg (183+ days/year, several years before death)
- Real estate in Vaud still taxed by Vaud (situs rule)

### Real Estate Consideration

**Scenario**: Own Vaud lakefront property, live in Fribourg

**Tax impact**:
- Vaud property → **Vaud taxes** at Vaud rates
- Movable assets (cash, stocks, business) → **Fribourg taxes** at lower Fribourg rates
- **Partial savings**: Movable assets benefit from Fribourg residence, but real estate remains expensive

**Strategy**: Sell Vaud property before death, reinvest in financial assets to shift tax jurisdiction to Fribourg.

## Vaud vs. Neuchâtel

### Tax Rate Comparison

**Neuchâtel characteristics**:
- **High-tax canton**, similar to Vaud and Geneva
- Smallest French-speaking canton
- Historically watchmaking and university town

| Relationship | Vaud Tax (CHF 1M) | Neuchâtel Tax (CHF 1M) | Difference |
|-------------|------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Spouse** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Child** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Sibling** | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | ~CHF 150,000 (15%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 10K |
| **Nephew/Niece** | ~CHF 220,000 (22%) | ~CHF 240,000 (24%) | Vaud cheaper by ~CHF 20K |
| **Unmarried partner** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 380,000 (38%) | Vaud slightly cheaper |

**Key insight**: Neuchâtel and Vaud are **very similar** in tax burden - both high-tax cantons with minimal difference.

### Strategic Implications

**When Neuchâtel offers NO advantage over Vaud**:
- Tax rates are nearly identical
- Relocation between Vaud and Neuchâtel provides **negligible tax savings**

**When to choose based on lifestyle only**:
- Both French-speaking
- Neuchâtel smaller, more provincial (University of Neuchâtel, watchmaking heritage)
- Vaud larger, more diverse (Lausanne urban center, wine country, lakefront resorts)

**Recommendation**: **Do NOT relocate** from Vaud to Neuchâtel for tax reasons (minimal benefit).

## Vaud vs. Valais

### Tax Rate Comparison

**Valais characteristics**:
- **Moderate-tax canton** (lower than Vaud, higher than Fribourg)
- Bilingual (French in lower Valais, German in upper Valais)
- Alpine canton, tourism and wine economy
- Popular for vacation homes (Verbier, Zermatt, Crans-Montana)

| Relationship | Vaud Tax (CHF 1M) | Valais Tax (CHF 1M) | Difference |
|-------------|------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Spouse** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Child** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Sibling** | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | ~CHF 100,000 (10%) | **Valais cheaper by CHF 40K** |
| **Nephew/Niece** | ~CHF 220,000 (22%) | ~CHF 180,000 (18%) | **Valais cheaper by CHF 40K** |
| **Unmarried partner** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 300,000 (30%) | **Valais cheaper by CHF 70K** |

**Key insight**: Valais is **moderately cheaper** than Vaud for non-direct heirs (CHF 40-70K savings on CHF 1M).

### Strategic Implications

**When to consider Valais over Vaud**:
- **Moderate estates to non-direct heirs** (savings justify relocation effort)
- **Lifestyle compatible**: Alpine lifestyle, outdoor activities (skiing, hiking)
- **Vacation home already in Valais**: Easier to establish residence if already own property

**Challenges**:
- **Lifestyle shift**: Alpine/rural vs. urban lakefront (Lausanne)
- **Language zones**: Upper Valais is German-speaking (cultural adjustment)
- **Accessibility**: More remote than Vaud in winter (mountain passes)

**Relocation ROI example**:
- Vaud resident, CHF 1,500,000 estate to nephew
- **Vaud tax**: ~CHF 330,000
- **Valais tax** (if relocate): ~CHF 270,000
- **Savings**: CHF 60,000

**Worth relocating?** Depends on lifestyle preference - savings are moderate but not massive.

### Vacation Home Ownership

**Common scenario**: Vaud resident owns Valais chalet (vacation home)

**Tax treatment**:
- **Valais chalet** → **Valais taxes** at Valais rates (situs rule)
- **Vaud primary residence** → **Vaud taxes** at Vaud rates
- Both taxes apply to respective properties

**Strategy**: If planning to retire to Valais chalet, relocate before death to shift movable asset taxation from Vaud to Valais (lower rates).

## Vaud vs. Bern

### Tax Rate Comparison

**Bern characteristics**:
- **Moderate-high tax canton**
- Largest canton, capital city (Bern)
- Trilingual (German majority, French in Jura bernois, some Italian)
- Borders French-speaking Vaud (Jura bernois region is French)

| Relationship | Vaud Tax (CHF 1M) | Bern Tax (CHF 1M) | Difference |
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Spouse** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Child** | CHF 0 | CHF 0 | Equal |
| **Sibling** | ~CHF 140,000 (14%) | ~CHF 120,000 (12%) | **Bern cheaper by CHF 20K** |
| **Nephew/Niece** | ~CHF 220,000 (22%) | ~CHF 180,000 (18%) | **Bern cheaper by CHF 40K** |
| **Unmarried partner** | ~CHF 370,000 (37%) | ~CHF 280,000 (28%) | **Bern cheaper by CHF 90K** |

**Key insight**: Bern is **cheaper** than Vaud for non-direct heirs, with significant savings (CHF 20-90K on CHF 1M).

### Strategic Implications

**When to consider Bern over Vaud**:
- **Estates to non-direct heirs** where tax savings justify relocation
- **Already considering German-speaking Switzerland**: Bern is gateway (capital, international presence)
- **Jura bernois option**: French-speaking region of Bern canton (maintains language, gains tax benefit)

**Jura bernois advantage**:
- **Cultural continuity**: French-speaking, similar to Vaud
- **Tax benefit**: Bern cantonal rates (lower than Vaud)
- **Proximity**: Borders Vaud, Neuchâtel

**Relocation ROI example**:
- Vaud resident, CHF 2,000,000 estate to unmarried partner
- **Vaud tax**: ~CHF 740,000
- **Bern tax** (if relocate): ~CHF 560,000
- **Savings**: CHF 180,000
- **Plus**: Registered partnership eliminates all tax (CHF 740K savings) - better strategy

**Note**: For unmarried partners, **registered partnership** (0% tax) is far more effective than relocation to any canton.

### Business Succession Opportunity

**Bern advantage over Vaud**: Bern offers **business succession relief** (up to 80% valuation reduction for qualifying family businesses).

**Vaud has NO business relief.**

**Example**: CHF 2,000,000 family business to nephew

**Vaud**:
- No relief, full taxation
- Tax: ~CHF 440,000 (22% effective rate on CHF 2M)

**Bern (with 80% relief)**:
- Tax base: CHF 400,000 (20% of CHF 2M after relief)
- Tax: ~CHF 72,000
- **Savings vs. Vaud**: CHF 368,000

**Implication**: **Business owners without direct descendants** should strongly consider relocating from Vaud to Bern (or other cantons with business relief) before succession.

## Multi-Canton Real Estate Portfolio Strategy

### Scenario: Property in Multiple Cantons

**Example**:
- Primary residence: Vaud (CHF 1,200,000)
- Vacation chalet: Valais (CHF 800,000)
- Investment apartment: Geneva (CHF 600,000)
- Total real estate: CHF 2,600,000
- Heir: Sibling
- **Current residence**: Vaud

### Tax Calculation (Current State)

| Property | Canton | Value | Tax Rate (Sibling) | Tax Owed |
|----------|--------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| Primary residence | Vaud | CHF 1,200,000 | ~16% | CHF 192,000 |
| Vacation chalet | Valais | CHF 800,000 | ~10% | CHF 80,000 |
| Investment apt | Geneva | CHF 600,000 | ~15% | CHF 90,000 |
| **Total** | | **CHF 2,600,000** | | **CHF 362,000** |

**Effective total rate**: 13.9%

### Optimization Strategy

**Option A: Relocate to Valais**
- Move primary residence to Valais chalet
- Sell Vaud primary residence (CHF 1,200,000), reinvest in financial assets

**New tax calculation**:
- Financial assets (CHF 1,200,000) → **Valais taxes** at sibling rate: ~CHF 120,000
- Valais chalet (now primary residence, in Valais) → **Valais taxes**: ~CHF 80,000
- Geneva apartment → **Geneva taxes** (unchanged): ~CHF 90,000
- **Total tax**: CHF 290,000
- **Savings**: CHF 72,000

**Plus**: Real estate gains tax on Vaud primary residence sale (factor this cost)

**Option B: Sell Geneva apartment before death**
- Sell Geneva apartment (CHF 600,000)
- Reinvest in financial assets
- Relocate to Valais

**New tax calculation**:
- Financial assets (CHF 1,800,000) → **Valais taxes**: ~CHF 180,000
- Valais chalet → **Valais taxes**: ~CHF 80,000
- **Total tax**: CHF 260,000
- **Savings**: CHF 102,000

**vs. Geneva property tax**: CHF 90,000 + real estate gains tax on Geneva sale (may offset savings)

**Optimal strategy**: Sell highest-tax canton properties (Geneva, Vaud), relocate to moderate-tax canton (Valais, Fribourg), convert real estate to financial assets.

## Alternative Strategies (Instead of Relocation)

### Strategy 1: Registered Partnership (Unmarried Couples)

**Scenario**: Vaud resident, estate to unmarried partner

**Instead of relocation to lower-tax canton**:
- **Register partnership** in Vaud
- **Cost**: ~CHF 200
- **Tax savings**: 100% (CHF 0 tax as registered partner)

**vs. Relocating to Fribourg**:
- Fribourg tax (unmarried): CHF 250,000 on CHF 1M
- Relocation cost: CHF 50,000+
- **Registered partnership in Vaud**: CHF 0 tax, no relocation

**Winner**: Registered partnership (massively superior)

### Strategy 2: Multi-Year Gifting (Stay in Vaud)

**Scenario**: Vaud resident, estate to sibling

**Instead of relocation**:
- Execute multi-year gifting program (see [Vaud Gift Tax Strategy](vaud-gift-tax-strategy.md))
- CHF 1M estate, gift CHF 250K every 6 years (4 tranches)
- Total tax: ~CHF 120,000 (vs. CHF 140K all at death)
- **Savings**: CHF 20,000

**vs. Relocating to Fribourg**:
- Fribourg tax: CHF 90,000 (CHF 50K savings vs. Vaud)
- Relocation cost: CHF 30,000+
- **Net benefit**: CHF 20,000

**Winner**: Relocation slightly better (CHF 30K net vs. CHF 20K), but gifting avoids lifestyle disruption.

### Strategy 3: Sell Vaud Real Estate, Keep Vaud Residence

**Scenario**: Vaud resident owns multiple Vaud properties

**Instead of relocation**:
- Sell investment properties (not primary residence)
- Reinvest in financial assets
- **Remain Vaud resident**

**Benefit**: Properties avoid high Vaud real estate situs tax (converted to movable assets taxed at Vaud movable rates - same)

**Limitation**: No tax savings (Vaud taxes movable assets same as real estate). Only benefit is liquidity/flexibility.

**Implication**: This strategy does NOT save tax in Vaud (unlike relocating to lower-tax canton where movable assets benefit from lower rates).

## Summary Recommendations

### For Direct Descendants/Spouses
**Tax impact**: CHF 0 in ALL cantons (exempt)
**Recommendation**: Choose residence based on **lifestyle only** - tax is irrelevant

### For Siblings (Moderate Estates CHF 500K-2M)
**Highest savings cantons**: Fribourg (CHF 50K+ savings on CHF 1M)
**Moderate savings**: Valais, Bern (CHF 20-40K savings)
**No benefit**: Neuchâtel, Geneva (similar or worse than Vaud)

**Recommendation**:
- **Consider Fribourg** if lifestyle compatible (French-speaking, similar culture, significant savings)
- **Consider Valais** if already have vacation home there (moderate savings, alpine lifestyle)
- **Stay in Vaud** if savings don't justify lifestyle change

### For Unmarried Partners
**Best strategy**: **Registered partnership** (CHF 0 tax everywhere) - far superior to relocation
**If registered partnership not option**:
- **Fribourg** offers largest savings (CHF 120K on CHF 1M vs. Vaud)
- **Bern, Valais** moderate savings (CHF 70-90K)

**Recommendation**: **Register partnership first** (eliminates all tax). Only consider relocation if partnership impossible.

### For Business Owners (No Direct Descendants)
**Critical**: Vaud has **NO business succession relief**
**Best cantons**: Bern, Zurich, Zug (80% valuation reduction)

**Recommendation**: **Strongly consider relocating** from Vaud to canton with business relief - savings of CHF 200-400K+ on medium-large business.

### For Multi-Canton Real Estate Owners
**Strategy**:
- **Sell properties in highest-tax cantons** (Geneva, Vaud, Neuchâtel)
- **Relocate to moderate-tax canton** (Fribourg, Valais, Bern)
- **Reinvest in financial assets** (taxed at residence canton rate)

**Example savings**: CHF 50-150K+ depending on portfolio size

## Limitations

### What This Framework Cannot Do

**This is a comparative educational tool, not relocation advice**:
- Tax is only one factor in residence choice
- Lifestyle compatibility, family ties, career opportunities often outweigh tax savings
- Cantonal rates change; verify current rates before decisions
- Individual circumstances may have unique considerations

### When Professional Consultation Is Mandatory

**You MUST consult professionals for**:
- Large estates (CHF 1M+) where relocation savings are substantial
- Multi-canton real estate portfolio planning
- Business succession planning (business relief qualification)
- International elements (foreign assets, dual citizenship)
- Registered partnership registration (unmarried couples)

### Verify Current Rates

**Before making relocation decision**:
- Confirm current cantonal tax rates (all neighboring cantons)
- Verify business relief availability and qualification (if applicable)
- Check registered partnership eligibility (if applicable)
- Consult multi-cantonal tax specialist for personalized analysis
